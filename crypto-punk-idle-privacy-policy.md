# Privacy Policy for Crypto Punk Idle

**Last updated: October 16, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Crypto Punk Idle** ("the Game", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you play the game.

Crypto Punk Idle is a pixel-art crypto-mining idle game for Android. All cryptocurrencies, prices, and markets in the Game are simulated: the Game does not mine, buy, sell, or hold any real cryptocurrency and has no connection to real wallets or exchanges. The Game works offline; the optional online features (cloud save and global leaderboard) use Google Firebase.

The Game is a paid app. It contains **no ads**, **no in-app purchases**, and **no analytics or tracking SDKs**.

## Information We Process

### 1. Game progress

The Game stores your progress on your device (money, hardware, upgrades, room items, coins, goals, fork tokens, daily bonus and missions, player name, and settings).

When an internet connection is available, a copy of this progress is also saved in **Google Firebase Cloud Firestore**, linked to an **anonymous Firebase account identifier** (UID) created automatically by **Firebase Authentication**. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, or password.

### 2. Leaderboard data

The Game has a global leaderboard. The following information is stored and is visible to other players:

- your chosen player name;
- your total earnings in the Game;
- your number of Hard Forks (prestige resets).

Choose a player name that does not reveal personal information.

### 3. Technical data

To provide authentication and cloud storage, Google Firebase may process technical information such as IP address, device and operating system information, and the anonymous UID. We do not use this data for advertising or profiling.

### 4. Purchase of the Game

The Game is purchased through **Google Play**. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never receive your payment details.

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, advertising ID, or any precise personal identifier. The Game does not request any dangerous Android permissions.

## Why We Process Data

We use information only to:

1. provide the Game and save and restore your progress across devices and reinstalls;
2. show the global leaderboard;
3. protect the service against abuse;
4. comply with legal obligations.

## Legal Bases Under the GDPR

- **performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))** to provide the Game, cloud saves, and the leaderboard;
- **legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f))** for security and abuse prevention;
- **legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c))** where processing is required by law.

## Service Providers and Data Sharing

We share information only with the providers needed to operate the Game:

- **Google Firebase** — Authentication (anonymous) and Cloud Firestore — [firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google Play** — distribution and purchase of the Game — [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

We do **not** sell or rent personal data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Information may be disclosed if required by law.

## International Data Transfers

Google may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers are protected by the safeguards provided by applicable law and by Google (for example, Standard Contractual Clauses).

## Data Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and protected by Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules, which only allow each anonymous account to write its own save and leaderboard entry. No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Data Retention

- **Local data** stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the Game.
- **Cloud save and leaderboard data** are kept while your anonymous account is in use or until you request deletion.

## Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your cloud save, leaderboard entry, and anonymous account by e-mailing **hoenngames@gmail.com**. We delete the data within 30 days. Full instructions are available here: [Crypto Punk Idle — Account and Data Deletion](https://github.com/dellarosamarco/policies/blob/main/crypto-punk-idle-account-deletion.md).
