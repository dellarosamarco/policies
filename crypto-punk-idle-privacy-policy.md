# Privacy Policy for Crypto Punk Idle

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Crypto Punk Idle** ("the Game", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you play the game.

Crypto Punk Idle is a pixel-art crypto-mining idle game for Android. All cryptocurrencies, prices, and markets in the Game are simulated: the Game does not mine, buy, sell, or hold any real cryptocurrency and has no connection to real wallets or exchanges. The Game works offline; the optional online features (cloud save and global leaderboard) use Google Firebase. When an internet connection is available, the Game also sends usage statistics and crash reports (see "Usage analytics and crash reports" below).

The Game is a paid app. It contains **no ads** and **no in-app purchases**. It uses **Firebase Analytics** and **PostHog** for pseudonymous usage statistics and **Firebase Crashlytics** for crash reports; none of these are used for advertising, and the advertising ID is not collected.

## Information We Process

### 1. Game progress

The Game stores your progress on your device (money, hardware, upgrades, room items, coins, goals, fork tokens, daily bonus and missions, player name, and settings).

When an internet connection is available, a copy of this progress is also saved in **Google Firebase Cloud Firestore**, linked to an **anonymous Firebase account identifier** (UID) created automatically by **Firebase Authentication**. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, or password.

### 2. Leaderboard data

The Game has a global leaderboard. The following information is stored and is visible to other players:

- your chosen player name;
- your total earnings in the Game;
- your number of Hard Forks (prestige resets).

Choose a player name that does not reveal personal information.

### 3. Technical data

To provide authentication and cloud storage, Google Firebase may process technical information such as IP address, device and operating system information, and the anonymous UID. We do not use this data for advertising or profiling.

### 4. Usage analytics and crash reports

**PostHog.** We use **PostHog** (PostHog, Inc., USA) for product analytics. PostHog processes:

- a **random device identifier** created by the PostHog SDK for this installation (it is not the advertising ID and is not linked to your name, e-mail, player name, or Firebase account);
- **usage events**: app installed, opened, updated, and sent to the background; screens and menus you view; the welcome screen and tutorial steps; items bought with in-game money (hardware, power, upgrades, furniture, perks) and coins unlocked; runs started and Hard Forks completed (run number, play time, in-game earnings); ranks reached; achievements; daily bonus streaks; menus unlocked; weekly events won; features used (for example crates, missions, overclock, golden blocks, bugs cleared, OTC deals, cosmetics, settings changes, and the rate button); rating prompts; notifications opened; and a few player properties (rank, number of Hard Forks, and whether the Pro boost is active);
- **device and app information**: device manufacturer and model, operating system and version, app version and build, language/locale, screen size, time zone, and network type;
- your **IP address**, which PostHog uses to derive an **approximate location** (country, region, city).

PostHog is used **without session replay or any recording of your screen**, and **no person profile** is created: events are linked only to the random device identifier. Events contain no name, e-mail address, player name, advertising ID, or text you type. We use one PostHog project for all our apps; each event is tagged with the app it comes from.

**Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.** The Game also sends similar in-game events and screen views to **Firebase Analytics** (with a Firebase app-instance identifier and device information; collection of the advertising ID is disabled), and crash reports (technical details of the error, device model, operating system and app version, and a Crashlytics installation identifier) to **Firebase Crashlytics**.

**Your choices.** The Game currently has **no in-game switch** to turn usage statistics off. You can stop all collection by uninstalling the Game, and clearing the app's storage or reinstalling the Game creates a new random PostHog identifier. You can object to analytics or ask us to delete your analytics data at any time by e-mailing **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

### 5. Purchase of the Game

The Game is purchased through **Google Play**. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never receive your payment details.

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, advertising ID, or any precise personal identifier. (PostHog derives only an approximate location from your IP address, as described above.) The Game does not request any dangerous Android permissions.

## Why We Process Data

We use information only to:

1. provide the Game and save and restore your progress across devices and reinstalls;
2. show the global leaderboard;
3. protect the service against abuse;
4. understand how the Game is used and improve it, measure how many players come back, find where players get stuck or stop playing, and fix crashes and other problems (usage statistics are never used for advertising and never sold);
5. comply with legal obligations.

## Legal Bases Under the GDPR

- **performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))** to provide the Game, cloud saves, and the leaderboard;
- **legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f))** for security and abuse prevention, and for usage analytics (PostHog and Firebase Analytics) and crash reporting, in our interest in understanding how the Game is used, improving it, and keeping it stable; you have the right to object to this processing at any time by e-mailing **hoenngames@gmail.com**;
- **legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c))** where processing is required by law.

## Service Providers and Data Sharing

We share information only with the providers needed to operate the Game:

- **Google Firebase** — Authentication (anonymous), Cloud Firestore, Analytics, and Crashlytics — [firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google Play** — distribution and purchase of the Game — [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **PostHog, Inc. (USA)** — product analytics — [posthog.com/privacy](https://posthog.com/privacy)

We do **not** sell or rent personal data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Information may be disclosed if required by law.

## International Data Transfers

Google may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers are protected by the safeguards provided by applicable law and by Google (for example, Standard Contractual Clauses).

**PostHog, Inc.** is based in the USA, and usage analytics are stored in PostHog's US cloud. These transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (included in PostHog's Data Processing Agreement) and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

## Data Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and protected by Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules, which only allow each anonymous account to write its own save and leaderboard entry. No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Data Retention

- **Local data** stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the Game.
- **Cloud save and leaderboard data** are kept while your anonymous account is in use or until you request deletion.
- **PostHog analytics events** are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted automatically (or anonymised/aggregated).
- **Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data** are kept according to Firebase retention settings.

## Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your cloud save, leaderboard entry, and anonymous account by e-mailing **hoenngames@gmail.com**. We delete the data within 30 days. Full instructions are available here: [Crypto Punk Idle — Account and Data Deletion](https://github.com/dellarosamarco/policies/blob/main/crypto-punk-idle-account-deletion.md).
