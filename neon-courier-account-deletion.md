# Neon Courier – Account and Data Deletion

**Developer:** MaarcoDev
**App:** Neon Courier (com.MaarcoDev.NeonCourier)
**Last updated:** November 2, 2026

Neon Courier does not require you to create an account with an email or password. The Game automatically creates an **anonymous account** used to save your progress in the cloud, which you can optionally link to Google or Apple sign-in.

## Delete your account from the Game

1. Open Neon Courier and go to **Settings**.
2. Open the **Account** section and tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm. Your account and cloud save are deleted immediately.

## Request deletion by email

If you can't open the Game anymore, send an email to **hoenngames@gmail.com** with the subject **"Neon Courier – Delete my data"**. Include your Player ID (shown in Settings) if you have it, or the email address of the Google/Apple account you linked. We will delete your account and associated data within **30 days** and confirm by email.

## What data is deleted

- Your account (Firebase Authentication user ID and any linked Google/Apple identity)
- Your cloud save (game progress (best scores, credits, unlocked couriers and boards, power-up levels, missions, settings)) stored in Cloud Firestore

## What data may be retained

- **Purchase records** are kept by Google Play / Apple App Store according to their own policies and legal obligations; we do not control them.
- **Aggregated analytics and crash reports** (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) are not linked to your identity and are automatically deleted according to Firebase retention periods (up to 14 months for analytics, 90 days for crash reports).
- **Advertising data** held by Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners is tied to your device advertising ID, not to your game account. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

## Local data

All data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game.
