# Emberfall Online – Account and Data Deletion

**Developer:** MaarcoDev
**App:** Emberfall Online (com.MaarcoDev.emberfall)
**Last updated:** November 25, 2026

Emberfall Online creates an **anonymous account** automatically (optionally linked to your Google account) to store your characters and let you play online.

## Option 1 – Delete from inside the Game

1. Open Emberfall Online and go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.
3. Your account, characters and associated data are deleted immediately from our servers (backups are purged within 30 days).

## Option 2 – Request deletion by email

1. Find your **Player ID** in **Settings → Account** (if you can still open the Game).
2. Send an email to **hoenngames@gmail.com** with the subject **"Emberfall Online – Delete my data"** and include your Player ID and character name(s).
3. We will delete your account and associated data within **30 days** and confirm by email.

If you cannot open the Game anymore, write to the same address with your character name(s) and approximate date of last play: we will do our best to identify and delete your data.

## What data is deleted

- Your account (Firebase Authentication user ID and any linked Google sign-in)
- Your characters and progress (level, items, gold, quests, friends, guild membership, settings) stored in Cloud Firestore and on our game server
- Your chat messages and server logs linked to your account

## What data may be retained

- **Messages already delivered** to other players may remain visible to them in their local chat history until it is cleared.
- **Records of reports and bans** may be kept for up to 12 months to prevent abuse and ban evasion.
- **Purchase records** are kept by Google Play according to its own policies and legal obligations; we do not control them.
- **Aggregated analytics and crash reports** (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) are not linked to your identity and are automatically deleted according to Firebase retention periods (up to 14 months for analytics, 90 days for crash reports).

## Local data

All data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game.
