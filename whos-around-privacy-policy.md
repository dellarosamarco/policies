# Who's Around — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how we process your personal data when you use Who's Around, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

## Data controller

Marco Della Rosa, Italy · hoenngames@gmail.com

## What we process

- **Account:** email and password (the password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it).
- **Profile:** name, date of birth (to check you are an adult and show your age), photo, bio, city, interests.
- **Location:** with your permission we use the device location to show nearby events and distances. We don't store your live location. If you create an event "around an area" we store only an approximate position (about 100 m); for venue events we store the venue's position.
- **Activity:** events you create, join requests, chat messages, favourites, ratings, reports and blocks.
- **Device:** push notification token, platform, language.
- **Purchases:** if you subscribe to Premium, subscription data from the store (not your payment details).
- **Technical data:** crash reports and anonymous or pseudonymous usage statistics, to improve the App.

## Why and on what legal basis

- Providing the service (account, profile, events, chat, notifications): performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
- Checking you are an adult, preventing abuse, handling reports and suspensions: legitimate interest in users' safety (Art. 6(1)(f)) and legal obligations.
- Managing Premium: performance of a contract.
- Statistics and diagnostics: legitimate interest in running and improving the App.
- Device location: your consent through the system permission, which you can withdraw at any time.

## What other users see

Name, age, photo, bio, city, interests, your average host rating and the events you publish. An online event's link and the chat are visible only to accepted people. We never show your email, full date of birth or live location.

## Who we share data with

- **Google (Firebase, Google Cloud):** hosting, database, authentication, notifications, diagnostics, acting on our behalf.
- **Google Play / Apple App Store:** subscription management.
- **OpenStreetMap:** map tiles and place search (they receive your IP address and search text).

Some providers may process data outside the EU; in that case the transfer relies on GDPR safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses).

## How long

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your profile, photo, events, memberships, requests, chats, favourites and notifications. We may keep reports and moderation records for a limited time when needed for users' safety or required by law.

## Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest by writing to hoenngames@gmail.com. You can withdraw location and notification permissions in your device settings. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

## Minors

The App is for adults only. If we find an account belonging to a minor we close it.

## Changes

We may update this policy; we will tell you in the App about important changes.
