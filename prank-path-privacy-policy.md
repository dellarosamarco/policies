# Privacy Policy - Prank Path

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Prank Path (package com.MaarcoDev.prankpath), published by MaarcoDev ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the app.

## Information we collect

Prank Path does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. Your game progress (unlocked levels, death counter, settings) is stored only on your device.

To keep the game stable and improve it, the app uses Google Firebase services that may automatically collect: anonymous usage data and app events (Firebase Analytics), crash reports and diagnostic data such as device model, OS version and stack traces (Firebase Crashlytics), and configuration requests (Firebase Remote Config). These services may process an app instance identifier and, where permitted, the advertising ID.

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed.

## How we use information

We use this data only to operate the game, fix crashes, understand which levels are too hard or too easy, and deliver purchased content. We do not sell personal data and we do not show advertising.

## Third-party services

Google Play Services, Google Play Billing, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config. Their handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data retention and deletion

Analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase default retention settings (up to 14 months for Analytics, 90 days for Crashlytics). Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress. You can request deletion of any data associated with your app instance by emailing us.

## Children

Prank Path is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Data is transmitted using encryption in transit (HTTPS) provided by Google services.

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at this URL with a new date.
