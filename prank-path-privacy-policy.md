# Privacy Policy - Prank Path

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Prank Path (package com.MaarcoDev.prankpath), published by MaarcoDev ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the app.

## Information we collect

Prank Path does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. Your game progress (unlocked levels, death counter, settings) is stored only on your device.

To keep the game stable and improve it, the app uses Google Firebase services that may automatically collect: anonymous usage data and app events (Firebase Analytics), crash reports and diagnostic data such as device model, OS version and stack traces (Firebase Crashlytics), and configuration requests (Firebase Remote Config). These services may process an app instance identifier and, where permitted, the advertising ID.

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed.

### Usage analytics (PostHog)

The app also sends usage statistics to PostHog, a product analytics service of PostHog, Inc. (USA). PostHog uses a random device identifier created for your installation (not the advertising ID, and not linked to your name or email) and receives: app installed, opened, updated and sent to the background; the screens you view (game, shop, stats, settings and similar); onboarding steps and tutorial completed; level started, completed or abandoned (level, mode, duration, deaths, first clear); daily level streaks; hints used; achievements; features used (skins, theme, language, haptics, settings actions, restore purchases); shop views; purchases started, completed, restored or failed (product, price, currency); and a few player properties (current level, levels completed, whether you own premium or the level pack). The PostHog SDK also adds device and app information (device manufacturer and model, OS and version, app version and build, language, screen size, time zone, network type), and PostHog receives your IP address, which it uses to derive an approximate location (country, region, city).

PostHog is used without session replay or any recording of your screen, and no person profile is created: events are linked only to the random device identifier. Events contain no name, email, advertising ID or text you type. We use one PostHog project for all our apps; each event is tagged with the app it comes from.

The app currently has no in-app switch to turn usage statistics off. Uninstalling the app stops all collection, and clearing the app's data or reinstalling it creates a new random identifier. You can object to analytics or ask us to delete your analytics data at any time by emailing hoenngames@gmail.com.

## How we use information

We use this data only to operate the game, fix crashes and other problems, understand how the game is used and improve it, measure how many players come back, understand which levels are too hard or too easy and where players stop playing, and deliver purchased content. We do not sell personal data, we do not use analytics data for advertising and we do not show advertising.

Where the GDPR applies, we process analytics and crash data (Firebase and PostHog) on the basis of our legitimate interests in keeping the game stable and improving it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You have the right to object to this processing at any time by emailing us.

## Third-party services

Google Play Services, Google Play Billing, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config. Their handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

PostHog, Inc. (USA) - product analytics - https://posthog.com/privacy

## International data transfers

PostHog, Inc. is based in the USA and usage statistics are stored in PostHog's US cloud. These transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (included in PostHog's Data Processing Agreement) and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Google may also process data outside your country under the safeguards described in its own policies.

## Data retention and deletion

Analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase default retention settings (up to 14 months for Analytics, 90 days for Crashlytics). PostHog analytics events are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted automatically (or anonymised/aggregated). Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress. You can request deletion of any data associated with your app instance by emailing us.

## Children

Prank Path is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Usage analytics are pseudonymous (linked only to random identifiers) and are not used for advertising or profiling.

## Security

Data is transmitted using encryption in transit (HTTPS) to Google services and PostHog.
