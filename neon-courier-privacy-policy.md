# Privacy Policy – Neon Courier

**Last updated:** November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Neon Courier** ("the Game", "we", "us"), developed by MaarcoDev, collects, uses and protects information when you play the Game on Android or iOS.

## 1. Who we are

Developer: MaarcoDev
Contact: **hoenngames@gmail.com**

## 2. Information we collect

We do **not** ask for your name, phone number, contacts or photos. To play, the Game automatically creates an **anonymous account** for you. You can optionally link the account to Google or Apple sign-in to keep your progress across devices; in that case we receive the email address and account identifier provided by that sign-in service.

| Data | Purpose | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous user ID, Firebase installation ID and (optional) Google/Apple account ID and email | Account, cloud save, analytics | Google Firebase (Authentication, Installations) |
| Game progress: game progress (best scores, credits, unlocked couriers and boards, power-up levels, missions, settings) | Cloud save and device transfer | Google Cloud Firestore |
| App interactions (screens visited, levels played, feature usage, ad and purchase events) | Improving game balance and features | Google Analytics for Firebase |
| Crash logs and diagnostics (device model, OS version, stack traces) | Fixing bugs and improving stability | Firebase Crashlytics |
| Purchase confirmations (product ID, purchase token) | Delivering in-app purchases and restoring them | Google Play Billing / Apple App Store |
| Advertising ID (Android AAID / iOS IDFA when permitted), IP address, device and app information, ad interactions | Showing ads, measuring ad performance, frequency capping and fraud prevention | Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its mediated ad networks |

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details.

## 3. Advertising

The Game is free to play and shows ads through **Unity LevelPlay** (Unity Technologies / ironSource), which may use other ad networks (mediation partners) to deliver ads. Ads include optional rewarded videos (you choose when to watch them) and interstitial ads at natural breaks. Purchasing "Remove ads", where available, disables interstitial ads.

Ad partners may use your advertising ID and device information to show ads, including personalised ads where you have given consent. In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through a consent form when you first open the Game, and you can change your choice at any time from the Game settings. On iOS, personalised ads are shown only if you allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings.

- Unity / LevelPlay privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
- List of LevelPlay mediation partners: https://developers.is.com/ironsource-mobile/general/ironsource-mobile-partners/

## 4. How we use information

- To save and restore your game progress across devices
- To deliver items you purchase and restore purchases
- To show ads and reward you for watching optional videos
- To understand how the Game is played and improve balance, content and performance
- To detect and fix crashes and errors

We do **not** sell your personal data.

## 5. Notifications

The Game may show **notifications** (for example, when the daily reward is ready). They can be disabled at any time in the Game settings or in your device settings.

## 6. Data sharing

Data is processed only by the service providers listed above (Google Firebase, Google Play, Apple App Store, Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners), which act under their own privacy policies:

- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Unity: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

We do not share your data with any other third party, except where required by law.

## 7. Data retention and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Cloud saves are kept while your account is active. Analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase default retention periods. Advertising data is retained by the ad partners according to their own policies.

## 8. Your rights and data deletion

You can delete your account and cloud data from the Game settings, or request access to or deletion of your data at any time. See the [Neon Courier Account and Data Deletion page](neon-courier-account-deletion.md) or write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**. Uninstalling the Game removes all locally stored data.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 9. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

## 11. Contact
