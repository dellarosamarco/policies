# Bitcoin Factory: Crypto Farm – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 16, 2026
**App:** Bitcoin Factory: Crypto Farm (Android package com.dellarosa.bitcoin_factory)
**Developer:** Marco Della Rosa
**Contact:** [marcodellarosa90@gmail.com](mailto:marcodellarosa90@gmail.com)

This Privacy Policy explains how Bitcoin Factory: Crypto Farm ("the app", "the game", "we") handles information. In short: **the game does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data.** It has no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting services, no accounts and no in‑app purchases.

---

## 1. The game is a simulation

Bitcoin Factory is an offline factory‑building game. All "Bitcoin", money, prices and markets in the game are **fictional virtual items with no real‑world value**. The app does not mine real cryptocurrency, does not use your device to mine, does not connect to any wallet or exchange and does not offer any real financial service.

## 2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (buildings, map, resources, research, missions, achievements, statistics) and your settings are saved **only locally on your device** using the standard Android app storage. This data never leaves your device and we cannot access it.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the app data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

## 3. Information we do not collect

The app does not request your name, e‑mail address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, camera or microphone. It does not use the advertising ID and it does not include third‑party SDKs for advertising, analytics or tracking. The game does not require an internet connection to be played.

## 4. Purchase of the app

Bitcoin Factory is a paid app. The purchase is processed entirely by **Google Play** under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your payment details. Google may provide us with aggregated, anonymous sales and installation statistics through the Google Play Console.

## 5. Sharing

Since we do not collect personal data, we do not share or sell any personal data with third parties.

## 6. Children

The game is a general‑audience game and is **not directed to children under 13**. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR in the EU/UK or CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because the app does not collect personal data, there is normally nothing for us to provide or delete; your local game data is fully under your control on your device. For any request, contact us at the address below.

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.
