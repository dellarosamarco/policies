# Privacy Policy – Lumigem

**Last updated:** November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Lumigem** ("the Game", "we", "us"), developed by MaarcoDev, collects, uses and protects information when you play the Game on Android or iOS.

## 1. Who we are

Developer: MaarcoDev
Contact: **hoenngames@gmail.com**

## 2. Information we collect

We do **not** ask for your name, phone number, contacts or photos. To play, the Game automatically creates an **anonymous account** for you. You can optionally link the account to Google or Apple sign-in to keep your progress across devices; in that case we receive the email address and account identifier provided by that sign-in service.

| Data | Purpose | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous user ID, Firebase installation ID and (optional) Google/Apple account ID and email | Account, cloud save, analytics | Google Firebase (Authentication, Installations) |
| Game progress: game progress (scores, adventure levels and stars, trophies, gem styles, power-ups, settings) | Cloud save and device transfer | Google Cloud Firestore |
| App interactions (screens visited, levels played, feature usage, ad and purchase events) | Improving game balance and features | Google Analytics for Firebase |
| Crash logs and diagnostics (device model, OS version, stack traces) | Fixing bugs and improving stability | Firebase Crashlytics |
| Random device identifier, app interactions (screens, games played, feature usage, ad and purchase events), device and app information, IP address (approximate location) | Understanding how the Game is used and improving it | PostHog, Inc. (USA) |
| Purchase confirmations (product ID, purchase token) | Delivering in-app purchases and restoring them | Google Play Billing / Apple App Store |
| Advertising ID (Android AAID / iOS IDFA when permitted), IP address, device and app information, ad interactions | Showing ads, measuring ad performance, frequency capping and fraud prevention | Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its mediated ad networks |

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details.

### Usage analytics (PostHog)

In addition to Google Analytics for Firebase, the Game sends pseudonymous usage analytics to **PostHog** (PostHog, Inc., USA):

- **A random device identifier** created by the PostHog SDK. It is not your advertising ID and is not linked to your name, e-mail or account.
- **Usage events**: app installed, updated, opened and sent to the background; screens viewed; games started and finished (mode — classic, daily challenge or adventure level —, score, duration, stars and why a game ended); tutorial progress; features used (for example hammer, revive, piece refresh, themes, weekly quests); achievements; daily challenge rewards; shop and offer views; completed purchases (product ID, price, currency); ads shown and rewards earned; sign-in events (sign-in method only); and a few game statistics (games played, levels completed, total stars, whether you made a purchase).
- **Device and app information**: device manufacturer and model, operating system and version, app version and build, language/locale, screen size, time zone and network type.
- **IP address**, which PostHog uses to derive an **approximate location** (country, region, city).

PostHog is used **without session replay** and does not record your screen. We never send your name, e-mail, advertising ID, account ID or free text to PostHog. Events are linked only to the random device identifier: **no user profile** is created in PostHog and your Firebase or sign-in account is not linked to these events.

## 3. Advertising

The Game is free to play and shows ads through **Unity LevelPlay** (Unity Technologies / ironSource), which may use other ad networks (mediation partners) to deliver ads. Ads include optional rewarded videos (you choose when to watch them) and interstitial ads at natural breaks. Purchasing "Remove ads", where available, disables interstitial ads.

Ad partners may use your advertising ID and device information to show ads, including personalised ads where you have given consent. In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through a consent form when you first open the Game, and you can change your choice at any time from the Game settings. On iOS, personalised ads are shown only if you allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings.

- Unity / LevelPlay privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
- List of LevelPlay mediation partners: https://developers.is.com/ironsource-mobile/general/ironsource-mobile-partners/

## 4. How we use information

- To save and restore your game progress across devices
- To deliver items you purchase and restore purchases
- To show ads and reward you for watching optional videos
- To understand how the Game is played and improve balance, content and performance, measure retention and find where players get stuck or stop playing (Google Analytics for Firebase and PostHog)
- To detect and fix crashes and errors

We do **not** sell your personal data. Analytics data (Firebase and PostHog) is not used for advertising.

**Legal basis for usage analytics (EU/EEA and UK):** usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase and PostHog) are processed on the basis of our **legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in understanding how the Game is used, fixing problems and improving it. The personalised-ads consent described in section 3 does not cover usage analytics. You have the **right to object** to this processing at any time (see section 8).

## 5. Notifications

The Game may show **notifications** (for example, when a new daily challenge is available). They can be disabled at any time in the Game settings or in your device settings.

## 6. Data sharing

Data is processed only by the service providers listed above (Google Firebase, Google Play, Apple App Store, Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners, PostHog), which act under their own privacy policies:

- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Unity: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
- PostHog, Inc. (USA) — product analytics — https://posthog.com/privacy

**International transfers:** some providers process data outside your country. PostHog, Inc. is located in the USA and stores analytics data in PostHog's US cloud; these transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (included in PostHog's Data Processing Agreement) and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

We do not share your data with any other third party, except where required by law.

## 7. Data retention and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Cloud saves are kept while your account is active. Firebase analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase default retention periods. PostHog analytics events are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted automatically (or kept only in anonymised, aggregated form). Advertising data is retained by the ad partners according to their own policies.

## 8. Your rights and data deletion

You can delete your account and cloud data from the Game settings, or request access to or deletion of your data at any time. See the [Lumigem Account and Data Deletion page](lumigem-account-deletion.md) or write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**. Uninstalling the Game removes all locally stored data.

**Usage analytics:** the Game currently has **no in-app switch** to turn usage analytics (Firebase and PostHog) off, and the personalised-ads choice in the Game's privacy settings does not affect them. Uninstalling the Game stops all collection; clearing the app data or reinstalling the Game resets the random PostHog device identifier, so new events are no longer linked to earlier ones. Deleting your account in the Game does not delete PostHog events, which are not linked to your account. To object to analytics or to ask for deletion of your analytics data, write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 9. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Analytics data is pseudonymous and is not used for advertising or profiling. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
