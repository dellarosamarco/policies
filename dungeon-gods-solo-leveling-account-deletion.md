# Account Deletion — Dungeon Gods: Solo Leveling

**Developer:** Marco Della Rosa — hoenngames@gmail.com

You can delete your Dungeon Gods: Solo Leveling account and associated data at any time.

## Option 1 — In the Game

1. Open the Game.
2. Go to **Settings → Delete account**.
3. Confirm.

## Option 2 — By e-mail

Send an e-mail to **hoenngames@gmail.com** with the subject **"Dungeon Gods: Solo Leveling – delete my account"**, including the hunter name shown in the Game (and, if you linked it, the Google account e-mail). We process requests within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Account (Firebase UID and, if linked, the Google sign-in link)
- Cloud save and game progress
- Server-side validation data
- Leaderboard entries and hunter name
- Local save on the device (Option 1)

## What may be kept

- Anonymous, aggregated analytics and crash data, kept according to Firebase retention settings (up to 14 months) and not linked to your account.

Purchase records of the Game are held by Google Play, not by us.
