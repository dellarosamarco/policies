# Loved — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how we process your personal data when you use Loved (the "App"), under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

## Data controller

Marco Della Rosa, Italy · hoenngames@gmail.com

## What we process

- **Account:** email address and password, or your Google / Apple sign-in. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
- **Profile:** name, date of birth (to check you are an adult and show your age), gender, who you want to meet, what you are looking for, interests, bio, job, city and up to 6 photos.
- **Sensitive data:** your gender preferences may reveal your sexual orientation. We process them only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which we ask for before collecting them.
- **Verification selfie:** if you request the verified badge, a selfie that a moderator compares with your photos. It is never shown to other users.
- **Approximate location:** with your permission we store only an approximate area (about 5 km) in a private record, to suggest people nearby. We never store your precise location and other users only see a rounded distance (never less than 1 km).
- **Activity:** likes, passes, super likes, matches, chat messages, read receipts, reports and blocks.
- **Settings and consents:** distance and age filters, incognito mode, notification preferences, and the date and version of the terms and consents you accepted.
- **Device:** push notification token, platform, language, and app integrity checks (Google Play Integrity / Apple App Attest) used to block counterfeit clients.

We do not use advertising SDKs and we do not sell your data.

## Why and on what legal basis

- Providing the service (account, profile, suggestions, matches, chat, notifications): performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
- Processing data that may reveal your sexual orientation: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)).
- Checking you are an adult, preventing spam, fraud and abuse, moderating photos, handling reports and suspensions: legitimate interest in users' safety (Art. 6(1)(f)) and legal obligations.
- Location and notifications: your consent through the system permissions, which you can withdraw at any time.

## Automatic photo moderation

Uploaded photos are automatically checked with Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch. Explicit or violent photos are deleted and removed from your profile, and you are notified. A person can review the decision if you write to us.

## What other users see

Your name, age, photos, bio, job, city, interests, what you are looking for, the verified badge and an approximate distance. Chat messages are visible only to the person you matched with. We never show your email, full date of birth or location. In incognito mode your profile is not shown in other people's suggestions.

## Who we share data with

- **Google (Firebase, Google Cloud):** hosting, database, file storage, authentication, push notifications, app integrity and photo moderation, acting on our behalf as processors.
- **Google / Apple:** if you sign in with Google or Apple, under their own privacy policies.

Some data is stored or processed outside the EU (for example, photos are stored in the United States). These transfers rely on GDPR safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

## How long

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your account, profile, photos, matches and messages. Reports and moderation records may be kept for a limited time for users' safety, to prevent banned users from returning, or when required by law.

## Your rights

You can access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw your consent at any time. You can download your data from Profile → Download my data, edit it from your profile, and delete your account in the App (see the [account deletion page](https://github.com/dellarosamarco/policies/blob/main/loved-account-deletion.md)). For any other request write to hoenngames@gmail.com. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by strict server-side rules: other users cannot list profiles or read your private data, and matches are created only by our servers.

## Minors

The App is for adults only (18+). If we find an account belonging to a minor we close it.

## Changes

We may update this policy. When we make important changes the App will ask you to review and accept them again.
