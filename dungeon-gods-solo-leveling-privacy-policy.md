# Privacy Policy for Dungeon Gods: Solo Leveling

**Last updated: October 28, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Dungeon Gods: Solo Leveling** ("the Game", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you play the game.

Dungeon Gods: Solo Leveling is a paid action RPG / roguelite for Android. The Game has **no ads and no in-app purchases**. Dungeons can be played offline; online features (cloud save, online leaderboards, daily challenge) use services provided by Google Firebase.

## Data Controller

- **Developer:** Marco Della Rosa
- **Privacy contact:** hoenngames@gmail.com
- **Country:** Italy

## Information We Process

| Data | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Account identifier (Firebase UID) | Cloud save, leaderboards | Created anonymously on first launch. No password is requested |
| Google account (only if you link it) | Restore progress on another device | Firebase Authentication receives your Google account ID, e-mail address and display name. Never shown to other players |
| Hunter name chosen in the Game | Shown on leaderboards | Max 16 characters. Do not use your real name |
| Game progress (level, items, records, runs) | Saving, server-side anti-cheat validation, leaderboards | |
| Anonymous gameplay events (e.g. dungeon started/completed, level reached) | Improving game balance | Can be turned off in Settings → Privacy |
| Crash reports and diagnostics | Fixing errors | Turned off together with gameplay statistics |

We **do not** collect location, contacts, photos, the advertising ID, or payment details. The purchase of the Game is handled entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment information.

## Why We Process Data

1. to provide the Game and save and restore your progress;
2. to show online leaderboards and the daily challenge;
3. to validate results and prevent cheating;
4. to fix crashes and improve game balance (can be disabled);
5. to comply with legal obligations.

## Legal Bases Under the GDPR

- **Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))** — cloud save, account, leaderboards;
- **Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f))** — anti-cheat, security, game-balance configuration;
- **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a))** — gameplay statistics and crash reports, which you can turn off at any time;
- **Legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c))** where required by law.

## Service Providers

- **Google Firebase** — Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config — [firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google Play** — distribution and purchase of the Game — [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

All data is transmitted encrypted (TLS). We do **not** sell personal data or share it for advertising.

## International Data Transfers

Google may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers rely on the safeguards provided by applicable law and by Google.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from **Settings → Delete account** in the Game: this permanently deletes the local save, cloud save, validation data and leaderboard entries. You can also request deletion by e-mail — see the [account deletion page](https://github.com/dellarosamarco/policies/blob/main/dungeon-gods-solo-leveling-account-deletion.md).

Analytics and crash data are kept according to Firebase retention settings.

## Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that such data was collected, we will delete it.
