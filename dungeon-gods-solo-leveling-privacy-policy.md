# Privacy Policy for Dungeon Gods: Solo Leveling

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Dungeon Gods: Solo Leveling** ("the Game", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you play the game.

Dungeon Gods: Solo Leveling is a paid action RPG / roguelite for Android. The Game has **no ads and no in-app purchases**. Dungeons can be played offline; online features (cloud save, online leaderboards, daily challenge) use services provided by Google Firebase. Usage analytics are sent to Google Firebase and PostHog.

### Usage analytics (PostHog)

We use **PostHog** (PostHog, Inc., USA) for product analytics. PostHog processes:

- a **random device identifier** created by the PostHog software when the Game is first started; it is not the advertising ID and is not linked to your account, Google account, name or e-mail address;
- **usage events**: the Game being installed, updated, opened and sent to the background; screens opened; dungeon runs started, completed, failed or abandoned (dungeon, mode, duration, kills, rooms cleared, what defeated you); daily challenges and live events (start, missions, rewards); onboarding and tutorial steps; features used (for example corruption, Play Games achievements, rating the Game); achievements, level-ups and rank-ups; daily rewards; in-game currency earned and spent; notification permission, notifications received and opened, and the notifications switch; rating prompts; account linked (Google or Apple, without any account details) or deleted; cloud-save conflicts;
- a few **game properties** used to group players: level, rank, number of runs started and dungeons cleared;
- **device and app information** added automatically: device manufacturer and model, operating system and version, app version and build, language, screen size, time zone and network type;
- your **IP address**, which PostHog uses to derive an **approximate location** (country, region, city).

PostHog does **not** record your screen (no session replay) and does not receive your name, e-mail address, Hunter name or the advertising ID. PostHog events are linked only to the random device identifier; no PostHog user profile is created.

**How to turn it off:** switch off **Settings → Privacy → "Share anonymous gameplay analytics & crash reports"**. This stops gameplay statistics (Firebase Analytics and PostHog) and crash reports; the choice is saved on the device. Uninstalling the Game stops all collection, and clearing the Game's data or reinstalling it resets the random identifier. You can also write to **hoenngames@gmail.com** to object or to request deletion.

We **do not** collect precise location, contacts, photos, the advertising ID, or payment details. The purchase of the Game is handled entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment information.

## Why We Process Data

1. to provide the Game and save and restore your progress;
2. to show online leaderboards and the daily challenge;
3. to validate results and prevent cheating;
4. to fix crashes, improve game balance, understand how the Game is used, measure how many players come back and find where players get stuck or stop playing (can be disabled). Analytics data is not used for advertising and is never sold;
5. to comply with legal obligations.

## Legal Bases Under the GDPR

- **Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f))** — anti-cheat, security, game-balance configuration;
- **Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f))** — gameplay statistics (Firebase Analytics and PostHog) and crash reports, to keep the Game stable and improve it. They are on by default and you can object at any time by turning them off in Settings → Privacy;

## Data Retention and Deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from **Settings → Delete account** in the Game: this permanently deletes the local save, cloud save, validation data and leaderboard entries. You can also request deletion by e-mail — see the [account deletion page](https://github.com/dellarosamarco/policies/blob/main/dungeon-gods-solo-leveling-account-deletion.md).

Analytics and crash data are kept according to Firebase retention settings. PostHog analytics events are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted automatically (or anonymised and aggregated).
