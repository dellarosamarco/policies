# Nexis — Account and Data Deletion

**Last updated: October 13, 2026**

This page explains how to delete your **Nexis: Notes, Tasks & Planner** account and the data associated with it.

Developer: Marco Della Rosa

## How to request account deletion

1. Send an e-mail to **hoenngames@gmail.com** with the subject **"Nexis – Delete my account"**.
2. Send it from the e-mail address linked to your Nexis account (or, for a guest account, include the account details shown in the app's account menu).
3. We may ask you to confirm the request to verify that you own the account.

We process deletion requests within **30 days** and send you a confirmation when it is complete.

## What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently delete:

- your Firebase Authentication account (user ID, e-mail, display name, sign-in provider);
- all your notes, to-do lists, spreadsheets, tasks, folders, and tags stored in Cloud Firestore;
- all your attachments (photos and audio) stored in Firebase Storage.

## What is kept

We do not keep any of your personal data after the deletion is complete. Data may remain in Google's encrypted backups for a limited period (usually up to 30 days) before being overwritten.

## Deleting only some data

You can delete individual notes, lists, spreadsheets, tasks, folders, and attachments at any time directly in the app, without deleting your account. Deleted items are removed from our cloud storage.

## Data on your device

Preferences and the offline cache are stored on your device. You can remove them by signing out, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app.
