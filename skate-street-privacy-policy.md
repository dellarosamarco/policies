# Privacy Policy – Skate Street

**Last updated:** 19 November 2026
**App:** Skate Street / Open Road (Android packages `com.maarcodev.openroad` and `com.maarcodev.openroad.pro`)
**Contact:** [hoenngames@gmail.com](mailto:hoenngames@gmail.com)

This Privacy Policy explains how Hoenn Games ("we", "us") handles information in **Skate Street** (the "App" or the "game").

**In short: the game does not collect your name, e-mail or any account data. It only sends pseudonymous usage statistics to our analytics provider, PostHog, to help us improve the game.**

- No account or sign-in is required.
- No advertising and no advertising ID. The only third-party service that collects data is **PostHog** (usage analytics, see section 2).
- No in-app purchases: all in-game currency is earned only by playing.
- The game can be played offline and does not need an internet connection; when your device is online, usage statistics are sent to PostHog.

## 1. Data stored only on your device

Your game progress and settings (levels, XP, coins, unlocked skaters, boards and cosmetics, missions, achievements, records, daily and weekly challenges, controls, sound and language settings) are saved **only on your device**. We cannot see or access this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data from your device settings.

## 2. Data we collect

The only data that leaves your device is **usage analytics**, sent to **PostHog** (PostHog, Inc., USA). Analytics is switched on when the game starts; the game currently has **no in-app switch** to turn it off (see section 6). PostHog receives:

- **A random device identifier** created by the PostHog SDK when the game is first started. It is not your advertising ID and is not linked to your name or e-mail.
- **Usage events**: app installed, updated, opened and sent to the background; screens viewed (menu, drive, map, season screen and similar); each drive started and finished (game mode, duration, kilometres driven, places discovered, top speed) and the end of the first-run tutorial; places discovered (place type and total count); speed traps and jumps (speed, distance, air time, whether it was a personal record); navigator destinations reached; timed deliveries started, completed or failed (reward, time limit, duration and the reason for a failure); cars bought (car and in-game price) and changed; features used (for example map, fast travel, camera view, radio station, weather, time of day, new world, language and graphics settings); and seasonal event challenges and rewards unlocked. A few game statistics are also kept as properties (places discovered, total kilometres, number of cars owned and the game language).
- **Device and app information** added by the PostHog SDK: device manufacturer and model, operating system and version, app version and build, language/locale, screen size, time zone and network type.
- **Your IP address**, which PostHog receives when events are sent and uses to derive an **approximate location** (country, region, city).

The game does **not** use session replay and does **not** record your screen. Events never contain your name, e-mail, free text or advertising ID. The game does not call PostHog's "identify" function, so events are linked only to the random device identifier and **no person profile** is created.

**Why we use it:** to understand how the game is used and improve it, measure retention, find where players get stuck or stop playing, and detect and fix problems. Analytics data is **not** used for advertising and is **never sold**.

**Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK):** our **legitimate interests** in understanding how the game is used, keeping it working and improving it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data is pseudonymous. You have the **right to object** at any time (see section 6).

**Retention:** PostHog analytics events are kept for up to **12 months** and are then deleted automatically (or anonymised/aggregated).

## 3. Permissions

The game may use vibration for gameplay feedback and uses internet access only to send the usage analytics described in section 2. It does not access your device location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or advertising ID; PostHog only derives an approximate location from your IP address.

## 4. Third parties

The game is distributed through Google Play. When you download it, Google processes data under its own [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The game itself contains one third-party service that collects data:

- **PostHog, Inc. (USA)** – product analytics – [Privacy Policy](https://posthog.com/privacy)

**International transfers:** PostHog, Inc. is based in the United States and analytics data is stored in PostHog's US cloud. These transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (included in PostHog's Data Processing Agreement) and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

## 5. Children

The game does not collect names, e-mail addresses or other directly identifying data from anyone, including children under 13. The usage analytics described in section 2 is pseudonymous (linked only to a random device identifier) and is never used for advertising or profiling.

## 6. Your rights

Your game progress is stored only on your device: you can remove it at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

**How to stop analytics:** the game currently has no in-app setting to switch analytics off. You can:

- **Uninstall the game**: this stops all further collection.
- **Clear the app data or reinstall the game**: this resets the random device identifier, so new events can no longer be linked to earlier ones.
- **Contact us** at [hoenngames@gmail.com](mailto:hoenngames@gmail.com) to object to analytics or to ask us to delete the analytics data linked to your device (we may ask you for details to locate it, since we do not know who you are).
