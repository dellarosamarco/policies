# Privacy Policy for Buby

**Last updated: September 25, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Buby** ("Buby", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app.

Buby is a location-based exploration app that lets users discover and track explored areas on a map. Depending on the features you use, Buby may process location information, map exploration progress, account information, settings, and technical data.

## Information We Process

### 1. Location data

Buby may request access to your device location in order to determine which areas you visit or explore and to update your map progress.

Depending on the operating system settings and permissions you grant, this may include:

- approximate location;
- precise location;
- latitude and longitude;
- timestamps associated with exploration events;
- derived map cells or geographic areas representing places you have explored.

Location access is used to provide the core exploration functionality. Buby does not sell location data.

### 2. Exploration and map progress data

Buby may store information such as:

- explored map cells or geographic areas;
- exploration percentages and progress;
- visited cities, regions, or countries where applicable;
- achievements, milestones, streaks, and related game progress;
- app preferences and settings.

Some of this information may be stored locally on your device. If cloud sync or account features are enabled, some information may also be stored online.

### 3. Account and authentication data

If Buby offers account or cloud features, we may process information such as:

- a user identifier;
- e-mail address;
- display name;
- authentication provider information;
- information returned by Google, Apple, or another sign-in provider when you choose to use it.

Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not stored by Buby in plain text.

### 4. Cloud data

If cloud backup, synchronization, social, leaderboard, or cross-device features are enabled, Buby may store account-related data and exploration progress using cloud services such as Firebase.

Cloud data may include the exploration and profile information described in this Privacy Policy.

### 5. Purchase and entitlement data

Buby may offer optional in-app purchases or subscriptions through Google Play and/or the Apple App Store.

To validate purchases, Buby may process platform/store, product identifiers, purchase tokens or transaction identifiers, and subscription or entitlement status. Payment details such as full card numbers are processed by the relevant app store and are not provided directly to Buby.

### 6. Technical and security data

Buby and its service providers may process technical information needed to operate, secure, and diagnose the service, including IP address, device type and operating system, app version, request timestamps, authentication events, and crash/error/service logs where enabled.

Buby does not sell personal data.

## Why We Process Data

We use information to provide explored-area tracking and map statistics; achievements and gameplay features; save preferences and progress; provide optional accounts, cloud backup and synchronization; restore purchases and premium entitlements; improve reliability and security; and comply with legal obligations.

## Legal Bases Under the GDPR

Where the GDPR applies, processing may rely on performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)), and legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)), as applicable.

You can change location permissions at any time through your device settings, although disabling location access may prevent core Buby functionality from working correctly.

## Service Providers and Data Sharing

Buby may use service providers such as Google Firebase, Google Play Services, Google Play, Apple App Store / StoreKit, and operating-system location services where applicable. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

We do **not** sell personal data. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

## International Data Transfers

Some service providers may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers are handled using safeguards provided under applicable law and by the relevant service provider.

## Data Retention

Local exploration data may remain on your device until you clear app data, reset the app, or uninstall it, subject to operating-system backup behavior. Cloud account and exploration data may be retained while your account remains active or until deletion is requested. Purchase and entitlement records may be retained as needed for validation, accounting, fraud prevention, or legal requirements.

## Account and Data Deletion

Where account or cloud functionality is available, Buby is intended to provide a way to delete account-related cloud data or request its deletion.

A public deletion guide is available in this repository as `buby-account-deletion.md`.

Deleting Buby cloud data does **not automatically cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription**. Store subscriptions must be managed through the relevant store account.

For deletion or privacy requests, contact **hoenngames@gmail.com**.
