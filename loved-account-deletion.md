# Loved — Account and Data Deletion

**Last updated: November 9, 2026**

This page explains how to delete your Loved account and the data associated with it.

## Delete from the app

1. Open **Loved** and sign in.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab and open **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm, entering your password again if asked.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. It removes:

- your account (Firebase Authentication user);
- your profile, photos and verification selfie;
- your private data (email, date of birth, approximate location, consents, settings);
- your likes, passes, matches and chat messages;
- your blocks and device registrations for notifications.

Before deleting, you can download a copy of your data from **Profile → Download my data**.

## Subscriptions and purchases

Deleting your account does **not automatically cancel a Google Play or Apple App Store subscription**. Manage or cancel it from your store account.

## If you cannot access the app

Request deletion by e-mail at **hoenngames@gmail.com** from the address you use to sign in, or include enough information to identify your account. Do **not** send your password. We may need to verify that you own the account. We complete requests within 30 days.

## What may be retained

Reports filed by or about the account and moderation records may be kept for a limited time for users' safety, to prevent banned users from returning, or where required by law. Data held independently by Google or Apple is governed by their own policies.
