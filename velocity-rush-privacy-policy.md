# Privacy Policy – Velocity Rush 3D: Arcade Racing

**Last updated:** 9 November 2026
**App:** Velocity Rush 3D: Arcade Racing (Android package `com.MaarcoDev.velocityrush`)
**Contact:** [hoenngames@gmail.com](mailto:hoenngames@gmail.com)

This Privacy Policy explains how Hoenn Games ("we", "us") handles information in **Velocity Rush 3D: Arcade Racing** (the "App" or the "game").

**In short: the game does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.**

- No account or sign-in is required.
- No advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No in-app purchases.
- The game works completely offline and does not need an internet connection.

## 1. Data stored only on your device

Your game progress and settings (levels, coins, cars, upgrades, colours, missions, achievements, best times and ghost laps, sound and language settings) are saved **only on your device**. We cannot see or access this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data from your device settings.

## 2. Data we collect

None. The game does not send any information to us or to third parties.

## 3. Permissions

The game does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or advertising ID.

## 4. Third parties

The game is distributed through Google Play. When you download it, Google processes data under its own [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The game itself contains no third-party services that collect data.

## 5. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

## 6. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal data, there is no data we can access, correct or delete on your behalf. You can remove all game data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data. If you have questions, contact us at the address below.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
