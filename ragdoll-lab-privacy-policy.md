# Privacy Policy – Ragdoll Lab: Physics Sandbox

**Last updated:** November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Ragdoll Lab: Physics Sandbox** ("the Game", "we", "us"), developed by MaarcoDev (Google Play developer account "Workout Master"), handles information when you play the Game.

## 1. Who we are

Developer: MaarcoDev
Contact: **hoenngames@gmail.com**

## 2. Information we collect

The Game can be played **offline** and does **not** require an account. We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise (GPS) location or advertising ID. The Game **contains no advertising**. It does include one analytics service, **PostHog**, which receives pseudonymous usage statistics when your device is online (see "Usage analytics (PostHog)" below).

| Data | Purpose | Where it is stored / processed |
|---|---|---|
| Game progress (unlocked items and maps, saved scenes, challenge progress, currency, missions, achievements, settings, language) | Saving your progress | Only locally on your device |
| Purchase confirmations (product ID, purchase token, order status) | Delivering and restoring in-app purchases | Google Play Billing |
| Usage analytics: random device identifier, gameplay and usage events, device and app information, IP address (used for approximate location) | Understanding how the Game is used and improving it | PostHog, Inc. (US cloud) |

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details.

### Usage analytics (PostHog)

The Game uses **PostHog** (PostHog, Inc., USA) for product analytics. Analytics is switched on when the Game starts; the Game currently has **no in-app switch** to turn it off (see "How to stop analytics" below). PostHog receives:

- **A random device identifier** created by the PostHog SDK when the Game is first started. It is not your advertising ID and is not linked to your name or email.
- **Usage events**: app installed, updated, opened and sent to the background; screens viewed; tutorial steps and whether the tutorial was finished or skipped; sandbox and challenge sessions played (map, challenge level, whether a saved scene was loaded); challenges started, completed or failed (duration, score, stars and the reason for a failure); features used (for example objects spawned, tools, slow motion, gravity, saving/loading a scene, unlocking items and maps, settings changes); XP level ups, missions completed and claimed, achievements unlocked and daily rewards claimed; shop views, purchase attempts, restored purchases and completed purchases (product ID, price and currency); your answer to the notification permission request; when you share a photo (only the fact that you shared one, never the image); when the rating prompt is shown; notifications opened; and technical error events (where the error happened and a short error message). A few game statistics are also kept as properties (player level, whether Pro is owned and the number of challenges completed).
- **Device and app information** added by the PostHog SDK: device manufacturer and model, operating system and version, app version and build, language/locale, screen size, time zone and network type.
- **Your IP address**, which PostHog receives when events are sent and uses to derive an **approximate location** (country, region, city).

The Game does **not** use session replay and does **not** record your screen. Events never contain your name, email, scene names, free text, photos or advertising ID. The Game does not call PostHog's "identify" function, so events are linked only to the random device identifier and **no person profile** is created.

## 3. How we use information

- To save your game progress on your device
- To deliver items you purchase and restore purchases
- To understand how the Game is used and improve it through usage analytics (PostHog): for example to measure retention, find where players get stuck or stop playing, and detect and fix problems

We do **not** sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. Analytics data is shared only with PostHog, which processes it on our behalf as a service provider.

### Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK users)

- Saving your progress and delivering purchases: performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- Usage analytics (PostHog): our **legitimate interests** in understanding how the Game is used, keeping it working and improving it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data is pseudonymous and is not used for advertising. You have the **right to object** to this processing at any time (see "How to stop analytics" in section 8).

## 4. Notifications

The Game may show optional **local notifications** (for example when your daily reward is ready). They are generated on your device, are only shown if you allow them, and can be disabled at any time in the Game settings or in your device settings.

## 5. Sharing screenshots

The Game lets you take a picture of your scene and share it. Sharing happens only when you choose it, through the standard Android share sheet, to the app you pick. We do not receive or store these images (usage analytics only records that a photo was shared).

## 6. In-app review

The Game may ask you to rate it using the Google Play In-App Review API. The review is handled entirely by Google Play; we do not receive your identity.

## 7. Third-party services

- Google Play / Google Play Billing / In-App Review: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- PostHog, Inc. (USA) – product analytics: https://posthog.com/privacy

### International transfers

PostHog, Inc. is based in the United States and analytics data is stored in PostHog's US cloud. These transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (included in PostHog's Data Processing Agreement) and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

## 8. Data retention, security and deletion

Game data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear the app data. There is no account, so there is no account to delete. Usage analytics events are stored by PostHog for up to **12 months** and are then deleted automatically (or anonymised/aggregated). For any request, write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

### How to stop analytics

The Game currently has no in-app setting to switch analytics off. You can:

- **Uninstall the Game**: this stops all further collection.
- **Clear the app data or reinstall the Game**: this resets the random device identifier, so new events can no longer be linked to earlier ones.
- **Contact us** at **hoenngames@gmail.com** to object to analytics or to ask us to delete the analytics data linked to your device (we may ask you for details to locate it, since we do not know who you are).

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are a California resident, you have the rights granted by the CCPA/CPRA; we do not sell or share personal information.

## 9. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Usage analytics data is pseudonymous (linked only to a random device identifier) and is never used for advertising or profiling. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
