# Privacy Policy for Ranked Workouts

**Last updated: September 24, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Ranked Workouts** ("Ranked Workouts", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app.

Ranked Workouts is a gamified strength, workout, personal-record, ranking, leaderboard, and training progress app. Some features work locally on the device, while optional cloud, account, leaderboard, friend, backup, and purchase features use online services.

## Information We Process

### 1. Local workout and profile data

Ranked Workouts may store data on your device such as:

- athlete name and profile preferences;
- bodyweight and unit preferences;
- workouts, exercises, sets, repetitions, loads, personal records, dates, and progress;
- ranks, levels, emblems, achievements, programs, templates, and app settings;
- local notification preferences and schedules.

This information can remain on-device when you use the app without cloud features.

### 2. Account and authentication data

If you use cloud features, Ranked Workouts may use Firebase Authentication. Depending on the sign-in method, we may process:

- a Firebase user identifier (UID);
- e-mail address;
- display name;
- authentication provider information;
- Google account information returned through Google Sign-In when you choose that option.

Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not stored by Ranked Workouts in plain text.

Ranked Workouts may also create or use an anonymous Firebase account when cloud functionality is enabled without a linked e-mail or Google account.

### 3. Cloud backup data

If cloud backup/sync is enabled, a private snapshot of your athlete data may be stored in Firebase Firestore. This may include the workout and profile information described above.

Private backup data is intended to be readable only by the account that owns it, subject to the app's Firebase security rules.

### 4. Public profile, leaderboard, and friend data

If you enable cloud/social features or publish records, some information is intentionally shared with other Ranked Workouts users. Depending on the feature, this may include:

- athlete name;
- unique handle;
- country code;
- motto;
- bodyweight or weight class where required for ranking;
- level and overall rank;
- exercise records, record values, record rank, and achievement date;
- leaderboard position;
- friend relationships.

Only publish information you are comfortable sharing with other users of the service.

### 5. Purchase and entitlement data

Ranked Workouts may offer in-app purchases or subscriptions through Google Play and/or the Apple App Store.

To validate a purchase, the app may process:

- store/platform;
- product identifier;
- purchase token or receipt information;
- subscription or entitlement status.

Payment details such as full card numbers are processed by Google or Apple and are not provided directly to Ranked Workouts.

### 6. Technical and security data

Service providers such as Firebase, Google, Apple, and the relevant app store may process technical information needed to provide, secure, diagnose, and operate their services, such as IP address, device information, operating system, request timestamps, authentication events, and service logs.

Ranked Workouts currently does not include an advertising SDK and does not sell personal data.

## Why We Process Data

We use information to:

1. provide workout tracking, personal records, ranks, achievements, and training features;
2. save settings and progress on your device;
3. create and manage optional accounts;
4. back up and restore your data when cloud features are enabled;
5. provide leaderboards, country rankings, weight-class rankings, and friend features;
6. verify purchases and provide premium entitlements;
7. provide password reset and authentication services;
8. protect the service against abuse, invalid data, fraud, and security incidents;
9. comply with legal obligations.

## Service Providers and Data Sharing

Ranked Workouts may share information only as needed to operate the service with providers such as:

- **Google Firebase** — Authentication, Firestore, and Cloud Functions;
- **Google Sign-In** — optional Google account authentication;
- **Google Play** — Android app distribution, billing, subscription/purchase processing, and purchase validation;
- **Apple App Store / StoreKit** — iOS app distribution, billing, subscription/purchase processing, and receipt validation.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

We do **not** sell personal data.

Information may also be disclosed if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

## International Data Transfers

Some service providers may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers are handled using safeguards provided by applicable law and by the relevant service provider.

## Data Retention

- **Local data** remains on your device until you reset it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app, subject to operating-system backup behavior.
- **Cloud account and backup data** is retained while the cloud account is active or until deletion is requested.
- **Public profile and leaderboard data** is retained while the related cloud data exists and may be removed when you unpublish records or delete cloud data.
- **Purchase entitlement data** may be retained as needed to validate or restore purchases and comply with store, fraud-prevention, accounting, or legal requirements.
- Google and Apple may retain transaction information independently under their own policies.

## Account and Data Deletion

Ranked Workouts provides an in-app option to delete cloud data/account information. The backend is designed to remove the user's authentication account and associated Ranked Workouts cloud documents, including athlete profile data, published records, leaderboard rows, private cloud data, and friend data.

A public deletion guide is available in the Ranked Workouts policies repository:

`ranked-workouts-account-deletion.md`

Deleting Ranked Workouts cloud data does **not automatically cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription**. Store subscriptions must be managed through the relevant store account.

For additional deletion or privacy requests, contact **hoenngames@gmail.com**.
