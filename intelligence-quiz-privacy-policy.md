# Privacy Policy – Intelligence Quiz: Brain Games

**Last updated:** November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile app **Intelligence Quiz: Brain Games** (package `com.dellarosamarco.intelligence_quiz`, the "App") handles your information. The App is developed and published by Workout Master ("we", "us").

## Summary

- The App works **completely offline**.
- We **do not collect, transmit, sell or share** any personal data.
- The App contains **no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases**.
- There is **no account** and no sign-in.

## Information stored on your device

To make the App work, some information is saved **only locally on your device** (using the operating system's app storage):

- Game progress: levels, stars, records, XP, trophies, streaks, missions and IQ test results.
- Preferences: the nickname and avatar you choose, language, theme, sound and vibration settings, and the time of the daily reminder.

This data never leaves your device and we cannot access it. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

## Leaderboards and rivals

Leagues and leaderboards are simulated on your device with virtual rivals. Your nickname and scores are not uploaded to any server.

## Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the App schedules a **local notification** on your device at the time you choose. No push service or server is involved. You can turn it off at any time in the App's Settings or in your device settings.

## Sharing results

If you choose to share a result, the text is passed to the app you select (for example a messaging app). We do not receive this content; the app you share with handles it under its own privacy policy.

## Permissions

The App may request permission to show notifications (for the optional daily reminder) and to schedule them at the chosen time. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

## Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

## Third-party services

The App does not include third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or tracking. When you download the App, Google Play may process data under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Data deletion

Since all data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
