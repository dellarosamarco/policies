# Artika — Account and Data Deletion

**Last updated: October 16, 2026**

**Developer:** Marco Della Rosa

This page explains how to delete the data associated with your **Artika** account.

Artika uses an anonymous Firebase account: no name, e-mail address, or password is required.

## How to request deletion

Send an e-mail to **hoenngames@gmail.com** with:

- the subject **"Artika data deletion"**;
- your in-game player name (shown in the FORK menu and on the leaderboard).

We may ask for additional details only if needed to identify the correct anonymous account.

## What is deleted

Within 30 days we delete:

- your cloud save (progress, hardware, upgrades, room items, goals, fork tokens, settings, player name);
- your leaderboard entry;
- your anonymous Firebase account.

## What may be kept

No game data is kept after deletion. Google Play may keep records of your purchase of the Game according to Google's own policies.

## Local data

Data stored on your device is removed when you clear the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → Artika → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the Game.
