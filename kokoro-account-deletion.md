# Kokoro – Account deletion

*October 2, 2026* · [Italiano](kokoro-account-deletion-it.md)

You can delete your Kokoro account and all associated data (coin balance, transaction history and all conversations) at any time:

1. Open Kokoro and go to the **Me** tab.
2. Tap **Delete my data** and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

If you no longer have access to the app, e-mail [hoenngames@gmail.com](mailto:hoenngames@gmail.com?subject=Kokoro%20account%20deletion) with the wallet address shown in the app (it starts with `kk_`); we will delete your data within 30 days. Purchase records may be retained as required by tax law.

See also the [Privacy Policy](kokoro-privacy-policy.md).
