# Pixel Heroes: Aura Farming – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **Pixel Heroes: Aura Farming** (the "Game") handles information. Contact: hoenngames@gmail.com

## 1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The Game does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Your game progress (heroes, currencies, settings and similar game data) is saved only locally on your device. It is never sent to our servers. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes this information.

## 2. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details or other billing information. Google may process data according to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. Ratings and reviews

The Game may show the standard Google Play in-app review prompt. Any review you submit is handled by Google Play under its own terms and privacy policy.

## 4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules local reminders on your device (for example when energy is full or idle rewards are ready). These are generated on the device and do not involve sending data to us. You can disable them at any time in the Game settings or in your device settings.

## 5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Third-party services

The Game relies on Google Play services (Google Play Billing and In-App Review), which are governed by Google's own privacy policy.

## 7. Data security and deletion

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data held by us to delete. All game data stays on your device and can be removed by uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

## 9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at **hoenngames@gmail.com**.
