# Privacy Policy – Monvex

**Last updated:** October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Monvex** ("the Game", "we", "us"), developed by MaarcoDev, collects, uses and protects information when you play the Game on Android or iOS.

## 1. Who we are

Developer: MaarcoDev
Contact: **hoenngames@gmail.com**

## 2. Information we collect

We do **not** ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos. To play, the Game creates an **anonymous account** for you automatically.

We collect only the following data:

| Data | Purpose | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous user ID and Firebase installation ID | Optional cloud save, analytics | Google Firebase (Authentication, Installations) |
| Game progress (creatures, items, badges, settings) – only if you enable cloud backup | Cloud save and device transfer | Google Cloud Firestore (servers in Europe) |
| App interactions (screens visited, battles played, creatures caught, feature usage) | Improving game balance and features | Google Analytics for Firebase |
| Crash logs and diagnostics (device model, OS version, stack traces) | Fixing bugs and improving stability | Firebase Crashlytics |

Monvex is a **paid game with no in-app purchases and no advertising**. The purchase of the Game is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never receive or store your payment details.

## 3. How we use information

- To save and restore your game progress across devices, if you enable cloud backup
- To understand how the Game is played and improve balance, content and performance
- To detect and fix crashes and errors

We do **not** sell your data and we do **not** show advertising in the Game.

## 4. Data sharing

Data is processed only by the service providers listed above (Google Firebase, Google Play, Apple App Store), which act on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

We do not share your data with any other third party, except where required by law.

## 5. Data retention and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Cloud saves are kept until you delete them or request deletion. Analytics data is retained by Google for up to 14 months and crash data for 90 days.

## 6. Your rights and data deletion

You can request access to, or deletion of, your data at any time. See the [Monvex Account and Data Deletion page](monvex-account-deletion.md) or write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**. Uninstalling the Game removes all locally stored data.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 7. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

## 9. Contact

For any question about this Privacy Policy: **hoenngames@gmail.com**
