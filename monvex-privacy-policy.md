# Privacy Policy – Monvex

**Last updated:** November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Monvex** ("the Game", "we", "us"), developed by MaarcoDev, collects, uses and protects information when you play the Game on Android or iOS.

## 1. Who we are

Developer: MaarcoDev
Contact: **hoenngames@gmail.com**

## 2. Information we collect

We do **not** ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos. To play, the Game creates an **anonymous account** for you automatically.

We collect only the following data:

| Data | Purpose | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous user ID and Firebase installation ID | Optional cloud save, analytics | Google Firebase (Authentication, Installations) |
| Game progress (creatures, items, badges, settings) – only if you enable cloud backup | Cloud save and device transfer | Google Cloud Firestore (servers in Europe) |
| App interactions (screens visited, battles played, creatures caught, feature usage) | Improving game balance and features | Google Analytics for Firebase |
| Crash logs and diagnostics (device model, OS version, stack traces) | Fixing bugs and improving stability | Firebase Crashlytics |
| Usage analytics: a random device identifier, gameplay and usage events, device and app information, IP address (used for approximate location) | Understanding how the Game is played and improving it | PostHog (USA) |

**Usage analytics (PostHog).** Unless you turn it off (see section 6), the Game sends usage analytics to PostHog, a product analytics service:

- a random identifier created on installation (it is not your advertising ID and is not linked to your name, email or anonymous account);
- usage events: game installed, updated, opened and closed; screens viewed; onboarding and tutorial steps; battles started and finished (battle type, mode, outcome, duration), creatures caught, evolved or released, trainers, gyms and the League defeated, story progress, badges and achievements; features used (such as the shop, PC, items, fast travel, daily quests and saving); daily rewards; store views and purchases (product, price and currency); and error events (where the error happened and a short error message);
- a few stable progress properties (such as badges earned, highest creature level and premium status);
- device and app information (device manufacturer and model, operating system and version, app version, language, screen size, time zone and network type);
- your IP address, which PostHog uses to derive an approximate location (country, region, city).

There is **no session replay or screen recording**, and no names, email or advertising ID are sent. The Game does not link PostHog events to your anonymous account: they are tied only to the random identifier above, and no personal profile is created in PostHog.

Monvex has **no advertising**. The purchase of the Game and any optional one-time in-game unlocks (such as the full adventure or the supporter pack) are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never receive or store your payment details.

## 3. How we use information

- To save and restore your game progress across devices, if you enable cloud backup
- To understand how the Game is played and improve balance, content and performance
- To detect and fix crashes and errors
- To measure retention and find where players get stuck or drop off (usage analytics, Google Analytics for Firebase and PostHog)

We do **not** sell your data, we do **not** use analytics for advertising, and we do **not** show advertising in the Game.

**Legal basis.** If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, we process usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase and PostHog) on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding how the Game is played and improving it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by turning off the analytics switch described in section 6.

## 4. Data sharing

Data is processed only by the service providers listed above (Google Firebase, Google Play, Apple App Store, PostHog), which act on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- PostHog, Inc. (USA) — product analytics — https://posthog.com/privacy

PostHog, Inc. is based in the USA and analytics data is stored in PostHog's US cloud. These transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (included in PostHog's Data Processing Agreement) and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

We do not share your data with any other third party, except where required by law.

## 5. Data retention and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Cloud saves are kept until you delete them or request deletion. Analytics data is retained by Google for up to 14 months and crash data for 90 days. PostHog analytics events are kept for up to 12 months and are then deleted automatically or anonymised/aggregated.

## 6. Your rights and data deletion

You can request access to, or deletion of, your data at any time. See the [Monvex Account and Data Deletion page](monvex-account-deletion.md) or write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**. Uninstalling the Game removes all locally stored data.

**Turning off analytics.** Usage analytics are on by default. You can turn them off at any time in **Options > Privacy** with the "Share anonymous usage statistics" switch: this stops both Google Analytics for Firebase and PostHog, and your choice is remembered. Uninstalling the Game also stops analytics, and clearing the Game's data or reinstalling it resets the random PostHog identifier. To object to analytics or request deletion of analytics data, write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 7. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it. Analytics data is pseudonymous and is not used for advertising or profiling.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
