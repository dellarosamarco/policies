# Monvex – Account and Data Deletion

**Developer:** MaarcoDev
**App:** Monvex (com.MaarcoDev.monvex)
**Last updated:** October 22, 2026

Monvex does not require you to create an account with an email or password. The Game automatically creates an **anonymous account** that is used only to save your progress in the cloud, if you enable cloud backup.

## How to request deletion of your account and data

1. Open Monvex and go to **Settings** to find your **Player ID** (shown in the profile/settings screen).
2. Send an email to **hoenngames@gmail.com** with the subject **"Monvex – Delete my data"** and include your Player ID.
3. We will delete your account and associated data within **30 days** and confirm by email.

If you cannot open the Game anymore, write to the same address and describe your device and approximate date of last play: we will do our best to identify and delete your data.

## What data is deleted

- Your anonymous account (Firebase Authentication user ID)
- Your cloud save (game progress, creatures, items, badges, settings) stored in Cloud Firestore

## What data may be retained

- **Purchase records** are kept by Google Play / Apple App Store according to their own policies and legal obligations; we do not control them. Monvex has no in-app purchases.
- **Aggregated analytics and crash reports** (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) are not linked to your identity and are automatically deleted according to Firebase retention periods (up to 14 months for analytics, 90 days for crash reports).

## Local data

All data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game.
