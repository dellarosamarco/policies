# Privacy Policy for Pixel Wilds: Survival Craft

**Last updated: November 12, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Pixel Wilds: Survival Craft** ("the Game", "we", "our") handles information when you play the game.

Pixel Wilds is an offline 2D pixel-art survival game for Android, sold as a one-time paid download on Google Play.

## Data Controller

- **Developer:** Marco Della Rosa
- **Privacy contact:** hoenngames@gmail.com
- **Country:** Italy

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.**

The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, no online multiplayer, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising. It does not request internet-related permissions for its own features and does not use the advertising ID.

### Data stored on your device

The Game saves your worlds, inventory, progress, statistics, and settings (such as language and audio) only on your device, using local app storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

### Google Play services

- **Purchase and licensing:** the Game is bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never receive your payment details. Google Play may check that your copy of the Game was obtained from Google Play.
- **In-app review:** the Game may show Google Play's in-app review prompt. Any rating or review you submit is handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

Google's processing of data is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Data Retention and Deletion

All game data stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the Game. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete; uninstalling the Game removes everything.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, including under the GDPR, you have rights over your personal data and may lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). For any question, contact **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

## Children's Privacy

The Game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game changes. The "Last updated" date above will be revised when material changes are made.
