# Privacy Policy for Memes: The Fight

**Last updated: October 9, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Memes: The Fight** ("the Game", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you play the game.

Memes: The Fight is a pixel-art 2D platformer for Android. The game works offline; the only online feature is the global leaderboard, which uses Google Firebase.

## Data Controller

- **Developer:** Marco Della Rosa
- **Privacy contact:** hoenngames@gmail.com
- **Country:** Italy

## Information We Process

### 1. Data stored on your device

The Game stores the following on your device only: your nickname, unlocked levels, best scores and stars, likes in your wallet, hats unlocked in the in-game shop, trophies, and settings (music, sound effects, vibration). This data is not sent to us.

### 2. Anonymous account

When an internet connection is available, the Game signs you in with **Firebase Authentication (anonymous sign-in)**, which creates a random anonymous user identifier (UID). We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, or password.

### 3. Leaderboard data

When you complete a level online, the following score entry is saved in **Google Firebase Cloud Firestore** and is **publicly visible** to other players in the leaderboard:

- your chosen nickname (up to 16 characters);
- level, character used, score, likes collected, enemies defeated, and completion time;
- your anonymous UID and the time the score was submitted.

Choose a nickname that does not reveal personal information such as your real name.

### 4. Technical data

Like any online service, Google Firebase may process technical information needed to deliver the service, such as your IP address and basic device information, in accordance with Google's privacy terms.

### What we do NOT collect

The Game does **not** use advertising, analytics, crash reporting, in-app purchases, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any device permission beyond internet access.

## Why We Process Data

We use information only to:

1. provide the Game and keep your progress on your device;
2. show the global leaderboard;
3. prevent abuse of the leaderboard and keep the service secure.

## Legal Bases Under the GDPR

Where the GDPR applies, processing relies on:

- **performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))** to provide the Game and the leaderboard;
- **legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f))** for security and abuse prevention.

## Service Providers and Data Sharing

We share information only as needed to operate the Game with:

- **Google Firebase** — Authentication (anonymous) and Cloud Firestore — [firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google Play** — distribution — [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

We do **not** sell or rent personal data.

Information may also be disclosed if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

## International Data Transfers

Google may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers are handled using the safeguards provided by applicable law and by Google.

## Data Retention

- **Local data** stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the Game.
- **Leaderboard entries** are kept while the leaderboard is active or until you request deletion.

## Data Deletion

To delete your leaderboard entries, e-mail **hoenngames@gmail.com** with your nickname (and, if possible, the levels you played). We delete the related entries within 30 days.

To delete local data, clear the app's storage or uninstall the Game.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, including under the GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

To exercise these rights, contact **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

## Security

We use reasonable technical measures such as HTTPS/TLS, Firebase Authentication, and Firestore security rules that only allow players to add their own scores. No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

Memes: The Fight is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that such data was collected, we will delete it.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game changes. The "Last updated" date above will be revised when material changes are made.
