# Wife AI — Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This page explains how to delete your **Wife AI** account (developer: Marco Della Rosa) and the data associated with it.

## Request deletion by e-mail

Send an e-mail to **hoenngames@gmail.com** with the subject **"Wife AI – Delete my account"** and include:

- the e-mail address of the Google account you use in Wife AI, **or**
- if you use the App as a guest, the user ID shown in the App's Profile screen (if available).

Do not send passwords or payment card information. We may ask you to confirm that you own the account before proceeding. Requests are completed within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- your Firebase Authentication account (Google-linked or anonymous);
- your profile (name, interests, age confirmation);
- all conversations, messages and photo requests;
- AI-generated images and custom characters you created;
- subscription/entitlement records and usage counters stored by Wife AI.

## Local data on your device

Some preferences are stored locally on your device. To remove them, clear the App's storage in Android settings or uninstall the App.

## Subscriptions and purchases

Deleting your account **does not cancel** an active Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Google may retain transaction records according to its own policies.

## What may be retained

Limited information may be kept where required by law, to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or meet app-store/payment obligations. Data held independently by Google (for example Google Play purchase records) is governed by Google's policies.
