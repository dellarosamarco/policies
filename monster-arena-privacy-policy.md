# Privacy Policy – Monster Arena

**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Monster Arena** ("the Game", "we", "us"), developed by MaarcoDev, collects, uses and protects information when you play the Game on Android or iOS.

## 1. Who we are

Developer: MaarcoDev
Contact: **hoenngames@gmail.com**

## 2. Information we collect

We do **not** ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos. To play, the Game creates an **anonymous account** for you automatically.

We collect only the following data:

| Data | Purpose | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous user ID and Firebase installation ID | Cloud save, analytics | Google Firebase (Authentication, Installations) |
| Game progress (monsters, items, currencies, stages cleared, settings) | Cloud save and device transfer | Google Cloud Firestore |
| App interactions (screens visited, battles played, feature usage) | Improving game balance and features | Google Analytics for Firebase |
| Crash logs and diagnostics (device model, OS version, stack traces) | Fixing bugs and improving stability | Firebase Crashlytics |
| Purchase confirmations (product ID, purchase token) | Delivering in-app purchases and subscriptions | Google Play Billing / Apple App Store |

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details.

## 3. How we use information

- To save and restore your game progress across devices
- To deliver items you purchase and keep subscriptions active
- To understand how the Game is played and improve balance, content and performance
- To detect and fix crashes and errors

We do **not** sell your data and we do **not** show advertising in the Game.

## 4. Notifications

The Game may show **local notifications** (for example, when your Monster Camp is full or daily gifts are ready). They are generated on your device and can be disabled at any time in the Game settings or in your device settings.

## 5. Data sharing

Data is processed only by the service providers listed above (Google Firebase, Google Play, Apple App Store), which act on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

We do not share your data with any other third party, except where required by law.

## 6. Data retention and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Cloud saves are kept while your account is active. Analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase default retention periods.

## 7. Your rights and data deletion

You can request access to, or deletion of, your data at any time. See the [Monster Arena Account and Data Deletion page](monster-arena-account-deletion.md) or write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**. Uninstalling the Game removes all locally stored data.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

## 10. Contact

For any question about this Privacy Policy: **hoenngames@gmail.com**
