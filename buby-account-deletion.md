# Buby Account and Data Deletion

**Last updated: September 25, 2026**

This page explains how users of **Buby** can request deletion of their account and associated cloud data.

## Delete your account from the app

Where account deletion is available in the current version of Buby, use the in-app account or settings controls to request deletion.

Deleting your account may remove cloud data associated with your Buby account, including, where applicable, account profile information, authentication-linked app records, cloud-synced exploration progress, explored geographic cells or derived exploration data, achievements, milestones, preferences, and social or leaderboard data.

## Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the in-app deletion option, send a deletion request to **hoenngames@gmail.com** with a subject such as `Buby account deletion request`.

Where possible, send the request from the email address associated with your Buby account. We may request limited information reasonably necessary to verify ownership of the account.

## Local data

Data stored only on your device may be removed by using any available reset/delete-local-data option in Buby, clearing the app's storage/data through device settings, or uninstalling the app. Operating-system backups may retain copies independently according to your backup settings.

## Location and exploration data

Buby may derive exploration progress from device location information. Deleting your cloud account is intended to remove exploration data stored by Buby in association with that account, except limited information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record purposes.

You can disable future location access at any time through your device's permission settings.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Deleting your Buby account or cloud data does **not** automatically cancel purchases or subscriptions managed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Recurring subscriptions must be cancelled through the relevant store account.

## Retention after a deletion request

We aim to delete or anonymize account-related Buby cloud data after a valid deletion request, except where limited information must be retained for legal/regulatory obligations, accounting or transaction records, fraud prevention, security and abuse prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement of legal rights.
