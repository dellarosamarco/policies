# Account and Data Deletion - Calisthenics Ranking: Level Up

Developer: Workout Master

You can delete your Calisthenics Ranking: Level Up account and all associated data at any time.

## Option 1: inside the App

1. Open the App and go to Settings > Account.
2. 2. Tap "Delete account and data".
   3. 3. Confirm. Your account, cloud backup and leaderboard entries are deleted immediately, and local data is erased from the device.
     
      4. ## Option 2: by email
     
      5. Send an email to hoenngames@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Calisthenics Ranking account" and include your in-app nickname (and the email of your linked Google/Apple account, if any). We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.
     
      6. ## What is deleted
     
      7. Account identifier, linked sign-in information, nickname, avatar, rank, level, XP, benchmark results, workout history and leaderboard entries stored in our cloud database.
     
      8. ## What may be retained
     
      9. Anonymous, aggregated analytics and crash reports may be kept by Google Firebase for up to 14 months. Purchase records are kept by Google Play according to its own policies. Active subscriptions must be cancelled separately in the Google Play Store.
