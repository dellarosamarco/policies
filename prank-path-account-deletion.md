# Data Deletion - Prank Path

Prank Path (com.MaarcoDev.prankpath) by MaarcoDev does not use user accounts. All game progress is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app data from Android settings.

## How to request deletion of your data

To request deletion of analytics and crash data associated with your app installation, send an email to hoenngames@gmail.com with the subject "Prank Path - data deletion". Include the app version and, if possible, the date you installed the game.

## What is deleted

Analytics events, crash reports and diagnostic data linked to your app instance in Firebase. Purchase records are kept by Google Play according to Google's own policies and can be managed from your Google account.

## Timing

Requests are processed within 30 days. Data not deleted on request is automatically removed according to Firebase retention (up to 14 months for Analytics, 90 days for Crashlytics).
