# Privacy Policy for Mappy

**Last updated: September 25, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Mappy** ("Mappy", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app.

Mappy is a location-based exploration app that lets users discover and track explored areas on a map. Depending on the features you use, Mappy may process location information, map exploration progress, account information, settings, and technical data.

## Information We Process

### 1. Location data

Mappy may request access to your device location in order to determine which areas you visit or explore and to update your map progress.

Depending on the operating system settings and permissions you grant, this may include:

- approximate location;
- precise location;
- latitude and longitude;
- timestamps associated with exploration events;
- derived map cells or geographic areas representing places you have explored.

Location access is used to provide the core exploration functionality. Mappy does not sell location data.

### 2. Exploration and map progress data

Mappy may store information such as:

- explored map cells or geographic areas;
- exploration percentages and progress;
- visited cities, regions, or countries where applicable;
- achievements, milestones, streaks, and related game progress;
- app preferences and settings.

Some of this information may be stored locally on your device. If cloud sync or account features are enabled, some information may also be stored online.

### 3. Account and authentication data

If Mappy offers account or cloud features, we may process information such as:

- a user identifier;
- e-mail address;
- display name;
- authentication provider information;
- information returned by Google, Apple, or another sign-in provider when you choose to use it.

Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not stored by Mappy in plain text.

### 4. Cloud data

If cloud backup, synchronization, social, leaderboard, or cross-device features are enabled, Mappy may store account-related data and exploration progress using cloud services such as Firebase.

Cloud data may include the exploration and profile information described in this Privacy Policy.

### 5. Purchase and entitlement data

Mappy may offer optional in-app purchases or subscriptions through Google Play and/or the Apple App Store.

To validate purchases, Mappy may process:

- platform or store;
- product identifier;
- purchase token, receipt, or transaction identifier;
- subscription or entitlement status.

Payment details such as full card numbers are processed by the relevant app store and are not provided directly to Mappy.

### 6. Technical and security data

Mappy and its service providers may process technical information needed to operate, secure, and diagnose the service, including:

- IP address;
- device type and operating system;
- app version;
- request timestamps;
- authentication events;
- crash, error, and service logs where enabled.

Mappy does not sell personal data.

## Why We Process Data

We use information to:

1. determine and display explored areas;
2. update exploration progress and map statistics;
3. provide achievements, milestones, and related gameplay features;
4. save preferences and progress;
5. provide optional accounts, cloud backup, and synchronization;
6. restore purchases and premium entitlements;
7. improve reliability, prevent abuse, and maintain service security;
8. comply with legal obligations.

## Legal Bases Under the GDPR

Where the GDPR applies, processing may rely on:

- **performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))** to provide requested app, account, cloud, and purchase features;
- **consent (Art. 6(1)(a))** for optional device permissions such as location access where required;
- **legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f))** for security, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and service integrity;
- **legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c))** where processing is required by law.

You can change location permissions at any time through your device settings, although disabling location access may prevent core Mappy functionality from working correctly.

## Service Providers and Data Sharing

Mappy may use service providers such as:

- **Google Firebase** — authentication, database, cloud storage, hosting, and backend functionality where used;
- **Google Play Services** — Android platform services and location-related functionality where applicable;
- **Google Play** — Android distribution, billing, subscription processing, and purchase validation;
- **Apple App Store / StoreKit** — iOS distribution, billing, subscription processing, and receipt validation;
- operating-system location services used to obtain device location with your permission.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

We do **not** sell personal data.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

## International Data Transfers

Some service providers may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers are handled using safeguards provided under applicable law and by the relevant service provider.

## Data Retention

- **Local exploration data** may remain on your device until you clear app data, reset the app, or uninstall it, subject to operating-system backup behavior.
- **Cloud account and exploration data** may be retained while your account remains active or until deletion is requested.
- **Purchase and entitlement records** may be retained as needed to validate or restore purchases and to comply with store, accounting, fraud-prevention, or legal requirements.
- Third-party providers may retain information independently under their own policies.
