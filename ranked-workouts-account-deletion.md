# Ranked Workouts — Account and Data Deletion

**Last updated: September 24, 2026**

This page explains how to delete your Ranked Workouts cloud account and associated cloud data.

## Delete from the app

If you are connected to Ranked Workouts cloud services:

1. Open **Ranked Workouts**.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Go to the cloud/account section.
4. Choose **Delete cloud data**.
5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

The Ranked Workouts backend is designed to delete the authenticated Firebase account and the cloud data associated with that user, including:

- athlete/public profile data;
- published personal records;
- leaderboard rows associated with the account;
- private cloud backup/snapshot data;
- premium entitlement data stored by Ranked Workouts;
- friend-list data owned by the account;
- the Firebase Authentication user.

The deletion action also disconnects the app from that cloud account.

## Local data on your device

Deleting cloud data does not necessarily erase data that is still stored locally on your device.

To remove local data, use the reset/start-fresh options available in Ranked Workouts where applicable, or clear the app's storage/uninstall the app. Device or operating-system backups may be subject to separate platform settings and retention rules.

## Subscriptions and purchases

Deleting your Ranked Workouts account or cloud data does **not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription**.

If you have an active subscription, manage or cancel it through the relevant app store account.

Google and Apple may retain purchase or transaction records under their own legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, and platform requirements.

## What may be retained

Ranked Workouts aims to delete account data associated with the service when a valid deletion request is completed. Limited information may still be retained where required by law, necessary to resolve disputes, enforce legal rights, prevent fraud or abuse, or comply with app-store/payment obligations.

Data independently held by Google, Apple, or other service providers is governed by those providers' own retention and deletion policies.
