# Privacy Policy – Lanternfall: Metroidvania

**Last updated:** 20 November 2026
**App:** Lanternfall: Metroidvania (Android package `com.maarcodev.lanternfall`)
**Contact:** [hoenngames@gmail.com](mailto:hoenngames@gmail.com)

This Privacy Policy explains how Hoenn Games ("we", "us") handles information in **Lanternfall: Metroidvania** (the "App" or the "game").

**In short: the game does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.**

- No account or sign-in is required.
- No advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No in-app purchases. The game is a one-time paid download.
- The game works completely offline and does not need an internet connection.

## 1. Data stored only on your device

Your game progress and settings (save slots, explored map, unlocked abilities, relics, collectibles, bestiary, achievements, speedrun times, difficulty, controls layout, sound, vibration and language settings) are saved **only on your device**. We cannot see or access this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data from your device settings.

## 2. Data we collect

None. The game does not send any information to us or to third parties.

## 3. Permissions

The game may use device vibration (haptics) and a connected Bluetooth game controller only locally, to provide gameplay feedback and controls. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or advertising ID.

## 4. Third parties

The game is distributed through Google Play. When you download it, Google processes data under its own [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The game may show the standard Google Play in-app review prompt; this is handled entirely by Google Play and we receive no personal data from it. The game itself contains no third-party services that collect data.

## 5. Children

The game contains fantasy combat against monsters and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## 6. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal data, there is no data we can access, correct or delete on your behalf. You can remove all game data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data. If you have questions, contact us at the address below.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
