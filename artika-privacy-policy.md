# Privacy Policy for Artika

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Artika** ("the Game", "we", "our") handles information when you play the game.

Artika is a top-down pixel-art open-world survival game for Android. The Game can be played **offline** and saves your progress on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, and no in-app purchases. When an internet connection is available, it sends **pseudonymous usage statistics and crash reports** unless you switch them off in the Game's settings (see "Usage statistics and crash reports" below).

## Information We Collect

We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, or any account details. The Game sends pseudonymous usage statistics and crash reports, described below, which you can switch off at any time.

### Game progress stored on your device

The Game saves your progress and settings **only locally on your device** (using Android's local app storage), for example:

- world seed, explored map, character level, experience, gold, inventory, and equipment;
- quests, daily missions, daily login streak, journal and statistics;
- settings such as language, music and sound volume, vibration, controls, and visual effects.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

### Usage statistics and crash reports

**PostHog.** The Game uses **PostHog** (PostHog, Inc., USA) for product analytics. PostHog processes:

- a **random device identifier** created by the PostHog SDK for this installation (it is not the advertising ID and is not linked to your name or e-mail);
- **usage events**: app installed, opened, updated, and sent to the background; screens and panels you view (for example menu, game, backpack, map, forge); games started and exited (new or continued game, character level, play time); tutorial steps and tutorial completed; character level-ups; dungeons entered and cleared (biome, dungeon level, duration); bosses defeated; quests completed; achievements; deaths (character level, biome, layer); daily login streak; seasonal event progress and rewards; gold spent and items sold in the Game (item, amount of in-game gold); features used (for example achievements, credits, settings); rating prompts; notifications opened; error events (where the error happened and the type of error); and a few player properties (character level and bosses defeated);
- **device and app information**: device manufacturer and model, operating system and version, app version and build, language/locale, screen size, time zone, and network type;
- your **IP address**, which PostHog uses to derive an **approximate location** (country, region, city).

PostHog is used **without session replay or any recording of your screen**, and **no person profile** is created: events are linked only to the random device identifier. Events contain no name, e-mail address, advertising ID, or text you type. We use one PostHog project for all our apps; each event is tagged with the app it comes from.

**Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.** The Game also sends similar game events and screen views to **Google Firebase Analytics** (with a Firebase app-instance identifier and device information; collection of the advertising ID is disabled), and crash reports (technical details of the error, device model, operating system and app version, and a Crashlytics installation identifier) to **Google Firebase Crashlytics**.

**Why and on what legal basis.** We use this data to understand how the Game is used and improve it, measure how many players come back, find where players get stuck or stop playing, and fix crashes and other problems. It is never used for advertising and never sold. Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is our **legitimate interest** in improving the Game and keeping it stable (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you can object at any time as described below.

**How to switch it off.** Usage statistics and crash reports are **on by default**. Open the Game's **Settings → Privacy** and turn off **"Anonymous stats and crash reports"**: this stops PostHog, Firebase Analytics, and Crashlytics at once, and you can turn it back on whenever you like. Uninstalling the Game also stops all collection, and clearing the app's storage or reinstalling the Game creates a new random PostHog identifier. You can also e-mail **hoenngames@gmail.com** to object or to ask us to delete your analytics data.

### No accounts or advertising

- The Game does **not** require registration or sign-in.
- The Game does **not** show ads and does **not** use the advertising ID.
- The Game does **not** request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

### Purchase through Google Play

Artika is a paid app. The purchase is handled entirely by **Google Play**, under Google's own privacy policy ([policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)). We do not receive your payment details. Google may provide us with aggregated, non-identifying sales and installation statistics through Google Play Console.

## International Data Transfers

**PostHog, Inc.** is based in the USA, and usage statistics are stored in PostHog's US cloud. These transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (included in PostHog's Data Processing Agreement) and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Google may also process data outside your country under the safeguards provided by applicable law and by Google.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the Game.

- **PostHog analytics events** are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted automatically (or anonymised/aggregated).
- **Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data** are kept according to Firebase retention settings.

To ask us to delete your analytics data earlier, e-mail **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, including under the GDPR, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). You can object to usage statistics and crash reports at any time by turning off **"Anonymous stats and crash reports"** in **Settings → Privacy**. For any other request or question, contact **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

## Children's Privacy

Artika does not ask anyone, including children, for personal data such as a name or e-mail address. Usage statistics and crash reports are pseudonymous (linked only to random identifiers) and are not used for advertising or profiling. The Game is not specifically directed to children under 13.
