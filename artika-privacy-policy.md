# Privacy Policy for Artika

**Last updated: October 16, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Artika** ("the Game", "we", "our") handles information when you play the game.

Artika is an offline, top-down pixel-art open-world survival game for Android. The Game works **completely offline**: it has no accounts, no online features, no ads, no analytics, and no in-app purchases.

## Data Controller

- **Developer:** Marco Della Rosa
- **Privacy contact:** hoenngames@gmail.com
- **Country:** Italy

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

### Game progress stored on your device

The Game saves your progress and settings **only locally on your device** (using Android's local app storage), for example:

- world seed, explored map, character level, experience, gold, inventory, and equipment;
- quests, daily missions, daily login streak, journal and statistics;
- settings such as language, music and sound volume, vibration, controls, and visual effects.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

### No accounts, analytics, or advertising

- The Game does **not** require registration or sign-in.
- The Game does **not** use analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
- The Game does **not** show ads and does **not** use the advertising ID.
- The Game does **not** request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

### Purchase through Google Play

Artika is a paid app. The purchase is handled entirely by **Google Play**, under Google's own privacy policy ([policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)). We do not receive your payment details. Google may provide us with aggregated, non-identifying sales and installation statistics through Google Play Console.

## Data Sharing

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not sell or share any personal data with third parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the Game. Since we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing else to delete.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, including under the GDPR, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). As the Game does not collect personal data, these rights are generally exercised simply by managing the data on your own device. For any question, contact **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

## Children's Privacy

Artika does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. The Game is not specifically directed to children under 13.

## Third-Party Assets

The Game uses graphics, music, and sound effects released under the CC0 license (credits are available inside the Game). These assets do not involve any data collection.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game changes (for example, if online features are added). The "Last updated" date above will be revised when changes are made.
