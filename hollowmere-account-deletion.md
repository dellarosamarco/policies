# Account and Data Deletion — Hollowmere: Hidden Clues

**Developer:** MaarcoDev · **App:** Hollowmere: Hidden Clues (`com.MaarcoDev.hiddenobjects`) · **Contact:** hoenngames@gmail.com
**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

Hollowmere: Hidden Clues does not ask you to register: it creates an **anonymous account** (a random Firebase user ID) only to back up your game progress in the cloud. You can delete this account and all associated data in two ways.

## Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

1. Open the Game and tap **Settings** (gear icon at the bottom of the home screen).
2. Tap **Delete my data**.
3. Confirm with **Delete**.

This immediately and permanently deletes:

- your cloud backup (game progress, coins, boosters, achievements, statistics, settings and the list of owned in-app products);
- your anonymous account (Firebase user ID);
- all game data stored on your device.

An internet connection is needed to delete the cloud part. If you are offline, local data is deleted and the Game asks you to repeat the operation when you are online.

## Option 2 — By email

If you no longer have the Game installed, or prefer us to do it:

1. Send an email to **hoenngames@gmail.com** with the subject **"Delete my data – Hollowmere"**.
2. If possible, include the date you last played and your device model, so we can identify your anonymous record. (We cannot see your name or email in the Game, because the Game never collects them.)

We will delete the cloud backup and anonymous account within **30 days** and confirm by email. Data stored only on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

## What is kept

- **Purchases:** payment records are kept by Google Play under Google's own policies. Non-consumable purchases (Detective License, Starter Pack) can be restored at any time with **Settings → Restore purchases** after reinstalling.
- **Analytics and crash reports** are not linked to your name or email; they are automatically deleted after their retention period (2 months for analytics user data, 90 days for crash reports).

## Resetting progress only

**Settings → Reset progress** restarts the story from the beginning but keeps your purchases and settings. It does not delete your account; use **Delete my data** for that.
