# Privacy Policy – Bastionfall: Tower Defense TD

**Last updated:** November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Bastionfall: Tower Defense TD** ("the Game", "we", "us"), developed by MaarcoDev (Google Play developer account "Workout Master"), handles information when you play the Game.

## 1. Who we are

Developer: MaarcoDev
Contact: **hoenngames@gmail.com**

## 2. Information we collect

The Game can be played **offline** and does **not** require an account. We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise (GPS) location or advertising ID. The Game **contains no advertising**. When your device is online, the Game sends pseudonymous **usage analytics** to PostHog (see "Usage analytics (PostHog)" below); this is the only analytics service in the Game.

| Data | Purpose | Where it is stored / processed |
|---|---|---|
| Game progress (levels, stars, gems, heroes, upgrades, quests, settings, language) | Saving your progress | Only locally on your device |
| Purchase confirmations (product ID, purchase token, order status) | Delivering and restoring in-app purchases | Google Play Billing |
| Usage analytics (random device identifier, game events, device and app information, IP address / approximate location) | Understanding how the Game is played and improving it | PostHog, Inc. (USA) |

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details.

### Usage analytics (PostHog)

The Game uses **PostHog** (PostHog, Inc., USA) to understand how it is played. PostHog receives:

- **A random device identifier** created by the PostHog SDK when the Game is installed. It is not your advertising ID and is not linked to your name or e-mail.
- **Game events**: app installed, updated, opened and sent to the background; screens viewed; battles started, won, lost or abandoned (level or endless biome, game mode, duration, stars or score, wave reached, bosses defeated); tutorial steps; features used (for example heroes selected or unlocked, upgrades, quests, settings changes); achievements; daily rewards; shop views and completed purchases (product ID, price, currency); rating prompts shown; notifications opened; and a few game statistics (highest level, stars earned, number of battles, whether you made a purchase).
- **Device and app information**: device manufacturer and model, operating system and version, app version and build, language/locale, screen size, time zone and network type.
- **IP address**, which PostHog uses to derive an **approximate location** (country, region, city).

The Game does **not** use session replay or record your screen, and it never sends your name, e-mail, advertising ID, free text or photos. Events are linked only to the random device identifier: **no user profile** is created and no account is linked to them.

## 3. How we use information

- To save your game progress on your device
- To deliver items you purchase and restore purchases
- To understand how the Game is used and improve it: measure retention, find where players get stuck or stop playing, and detect and fix problems (usage analytics)

We do **not** sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. Analytics data is shared only with PostHog, which processes it on our behalf.

**Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK):** usage analytics are processed on the basis of our **legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in understanding how the Game is used, keeping it working and improving it. The data is pseudonymous and is not used for advertising. You have the **right to object** at any time (see section 7).

## 4. Notifications

The Game may show optional **local notifications** (for example when your daily reward is ready). They are generated on your device, are only shown if you allow them, and can be disabled at any time in the Game settings or in your device settings.

## 5. In-app review

The Game may ask you to rate it using the Google Play In-App Review API. The review is handled entirely by Google Play; we do not receive your identity.

## 6. Third-party services

- Google Play / Google Play Billing / In-App Review: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- PostHog, Inc. (USA) — product analytics — https://posthog.com/privacy

**International transfers:** PostHog, Inc. is located in the USA and analytics data is stored in PostHog's US cloud. These transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (included in PostHog's Data Processing Agreement) and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

## 7. Data retention, security and deletion

Game data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear the app data. The Game has no accounts, so there is no account to delete.

Usage analytics events are kept by PostHog for up to **12 months** and are then deleted automatically (or kept only in anonymised, aggregated form).

**How to stop analytics:** the Game currently has **no in-app switch** to turn analytics off. Uninstalling the Game stops all collection, and clearing the app data or reinstalling the Game resets the random device identifier (new events are no longer linked to the old ones). To object to analytics or to ask for your analytics data to be deleted, write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

For any other request, write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are a California resident, you have the rights granted by the CCPA/CPRA; we do not sell or share personal information.

## 8. Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Usage analytics are pseudonymous (linked only to a random device identifier) and are never used for advertising or profiling. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
