# Privacy Policy – Bastionfall: Tower Defense TD

**Last updated:** November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Bastionfall: Tower Defense TD** ("the Game", "we", "us"), developed by MaarcoDev (Google Play developer account "Workout Master"), handles information when you play the Game.

## 1. Who we are

Developer: MaarcoDev
Contact: **hoenngames@gmail.com**

## 2. Information we collect

The Game works **offline** and does **not** require an account. We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or advertising ID. The Game **contains no advertising** and includes **no analytics or tracking SDKs**.

| Data | Purpose | Where it is stored / processed |
|---|---|---|
| Game progress (levels, stars, gems, heroes, upgrades, quests, settings, language) | Saving your progress | Only locally on your device |
| Purchase confirmations (product ID, purchase token, order status) | Delivering and restoring in-app purchases | Google Play Billing |

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details.

## 3. How we use information

- To save your game progress on your device
- To deliver items you purchase and restore purchases

We do **not** sell or share your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

## 4. Notifications

The Game may show optional **local notifications** (for example when your daily reward is ready). They are generated on your device, are only shown if you allow them, and can be disabled at any time in the Game settings or in your device settings.

## 5. In-app review

The Game may ask you to rate it using the Google Play In-App Review API. The review is handled entirely by Google Play; we do not receive your identity.

## 6. Third-party services

- Google Play / Google Play Billing / In-App Review: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 7. Data retention, security and deletion

Game data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear the app data. Since we do not hold any account or personal data on our servers, there is no account to delete. For any request, write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are a California resident, you have the rights granted by the CCPA/CPRA; we do not sell or share personal information.

## 8. Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

## 10. Contact
