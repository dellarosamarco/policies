# AI Match – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 23, 2026*

This policy explains what data the AI Match app ("AI Match", "we") processes, why, and your rights. The data controller is **Marco Della Rosa**, contact: [hoenngames@gmail.com](mailto:hoenngames@gmail.com).

AI Match does not require an account. We do not ask for your e-mail, phone number or real identity.

## 1. Data we process

- **Profile you enter**: a first name or nickname, your age and the interests you pick. They are stored **only on your device** and used to personalise the characters' replies.
- **Chat content**: the messages you send to the characters and their AI-generated replies. Conversations are stored **only on your device**. To generate each reply, your latest messages, your nickname and interests and the character's persona are sent to Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic.
- **Game data**: matches, interest levels, coins, daily counters and settings, stored only on your device.
- **Reports**: when you long-press a message and report it, the message and the reason are stored on your device and the message is removed from the chat. You can also send reports to us by e-mail.
- **Purchase data**: Google Play handles payments. The app receives a purchase confirmation to unlock Premium or add coins. We never receive your payment card details.

## 2. Why we process it

- To provide the game you request: AI replies, matches, purchases (performance of a contract).
- To keep the service safe and prevent abuse (legitimate interest).

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads and we do not use your conversations to train AI models.

## 3. Service providers

- **Google Firebase AI Logic / Gemini API** (Google LLC): generates the characters' replies. Data sent to Google is processed under the Gemini API and Firebase terms.
- **Google Play**: payments and subscriptions.

Some providers process data outside the EU/EEA (e.g. in the United States) under appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## 4. Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays there until you delete it. You can delete everything at any time in the app (*Profile → Delete my data*) or by uninstalling the app. We do not keep copies of your conversations on our own servers.

## 5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. under the GDPR) you have the right to access, rectify, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data and to data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy: the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). Write to [hoenngames@gmail.com](mailto:hoenngames@gmail.com).

## 6. Age requirement

AI Match is intended only for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly process data of minors.

## 7. AI-generated content

All characters are fictional and their replies are generated by artificial intelligence. AI Match is not a dating service and does not connect you with real people. Replies may be inaccurate. Do not share sensitive personal information in chats.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app.
