# AI Match – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 23, 2026*

This policy explains what data the AI Match app ("AI Match", "we") processes, why, and your rights. The data controller is **Marco Della Rosa**, contact: [hoenngames@gmail.com](mailto:hoenngames@gmail.com).

AI Match does not require an account. We do not ask for your e-mail, phone number or real identity.

## 1. Data we process

- **Anonymous ID**: when you open the app we create an anonymous Firebase Authentication ID. It is not linked to your name, e-mail or phone number.
- **Profile you enter**: a first name or nickname, your age and the interests you pick. They are stored on your device. With each chat message your nickname, age and interests are sent to our server and to Google Gemini to personalise the reply; the server does not store them.
- **Birth year**: when you confirm you are 18 or older, the birth year is stored on our server with your anonymous ID.
- **Chat content**: the messages you send to the characters and their AI-generated replies are stored on your device and on our server (Google Cloud Firestore, region europe-west1), linked to your anonymous ID. To generate each reply, your recent messages and the character's persona are sent to Google's Gemini models through Vertex AI.
- **Photos and voice notes**: photos you ask a character for are generated by Gemini and stored in Firebase Cloud Storage in a folder that only your anonymous ID can read; a copy is saved on your device. Voice notes are generated on request and saved only on your device.
- **Game data**: coin balance, daily usage counters (free messages, likes) and purchase records are stored on our server so coins cannot be lost or faked. Matches, interest levels and settings are stored on your device.
- **App integrity**: we use Firebase App Check (Google Play Integrity) to confirm requests come from the genuine app.
- **Reports**: when you long-press a message and report it, the message and the reason are stored on your device and the message is removed from the chat. You can also send reports to us by e-mail.
- **Purchase data**: Google Play handles payments. Our server receives the purchase token to verify it with Google Play and add coins. We never receive your payment card details.

## 2. Why we process it

- To provide the game you request: AI replies, photos, voice notes, matches and coins (performance of a contract).
- To keep the service safe, verify purchases and prevent abuse (legitimate interest).

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads and we do not use your conversations to train AI models.

## 3. Service providers

- **Google Cloud / Firebase** (Google LLC): anonymous authentication, Cloud Functions, Firestore database and Cloud Storage, hosted in the EU (europe-west1), and App Check.
- **Google Vertex AI / Gemini** (Google LLC): generates the characters' replies, photos and voice notes. Under Google Cloud terms, this data is not used to train Google's models.
- **Google Play**: payments.

Some providers may process data outside the EU/EEA (e.g. in the United States) under appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## 4. Retention and deletion

Server data (chats, photos, birth year, coins and counters) is kept while you use the app. You can delete it at any time in the app (*Profile → Delete my data*): this erases the data on your device and permanently deletes your chats, photos and birth year from our server. Your coin balance and purchase records are kept with your anonymous ID so that coins you bought are not lost, and purchase records may be kept as long as required by tax law. If you do not use the app for 12 months, all server data linked to your anonymous ID is deleted automatically, including chats, photos and any unspent coins, also purchased ones. Purchase records (anonymous ID, product, Google Play order number and date, with no chat content) are kept as accounting records for as long as tax law requires, also after deletion.

## 5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. under the GDPR) you have the right to access, rectify, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data and to data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy: the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). Write to [hoenngames@gmail.com](mailto:hoenngames@gmail.com).

## 6. Age requirement

AI Match is intended only for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly process data of minors.

## 7. AI-generated content

All characters are fictional and their replies are generated by artificial intelligence. AI Match is not a dating service and does not connect you with real people. Replies may be inaccurate. Do not share sensitive personal information in chats.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app.
