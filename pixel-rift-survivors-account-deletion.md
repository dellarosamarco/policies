# Pixel Rift: Survivors — Account and Data Deletion

**Last updated: October 3, 2026**

**Developer:** Marco Della Rosa

This page explains how to delete the data associated with your Pixel Rift: Survivors account.

Pixel Rift: Survivors uses an anonymous Firebase account: no name, e-mail address, or password is required.

## Delete from the game

1. Open **Pixel Rift: Survivors**.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete my data**.
4. Confirm the deletion.

## Delete by e-mail

If you no longer have the game installed, send an e-mail to **hoenngames@gmail.com** with the subject "Pixel Rift data deletion" and your in-game nickname.

## What is deleted

Within 30 days we delete:

- your cloud save (coins, gems, upgrades, unlocked heroes and maps, records, settings, nickname);
- your leaderboard entries;
- your anonymous Firebase account.

## What may be kept

- Purchase records needed to meet accounting, fraud-prevention, or legal obligations, for up to 10 years where the law requires it.
- Analytics and crash data, which are not linked to your identity and are kept according to Firebase retention settings.

## Local data

Data stored on your device is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game.

## Subscriptions

Deleting your data does **not** cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel it from the Google Play Store.
