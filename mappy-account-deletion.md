# Mappy Account and Data Deletion

**Last updated: September 25, 2026**

This page explains how users of **Mappy** can request deletion of their account and associated cloud data.

## Delete your account from the app

Where account deletion is available in the current version of Mappy, you can use the in-app account or settings controls to request deletion.

The exact labels may vary by app version, but the flow is intended to be available from the account/settings area.

Deleting your account may remove cloud data associated with your Mappy account, including, where applicable:

- account profile information;
- authentication-linked app records;
- cloud-synced exploration progress;
- explored geographic cells or derived exploration data stored online;
- achievements, milestones, preferences, and other cloud-synced app data;
- social or leaderboard data associated with the account, where such features exist.

## Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the in-app deletion option, send a deletion request to:

**hoenngames@gmail.com**

Use a subject such as:

`Mappy account deletion request`

Please send the request from the email address associated with your Mappy account where possible. We may ask for limited additional information when reasonably necessary to verify that the request relates to your account.

## Local data

Data stored only on your device may be removed by:

- using any available reset/delete-local-data option in Mappy;
- clearing the app's storage/data through your device settings; or
- uninstalling the app.

Operating-system backups may retain copies independently according to your device and backup settings.

## Location and exploration data

Mappy may derive exploration progress from device location information. Deleting your cloud account is intended to remove exploration data stored by Mappy in association with that account, subject to any limited information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record purposes.

You can separately disable future location access at any time through your device's permission settings.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Deleting your Mappy account or cloud data does **not** automatically cancel purchases or subscriptions managed by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

To stop a recurring subscription, cancel it through the relevant store account.

Google and Apple may retain transaction information under their own legal obligations and privacy policies.

## Retention after a deletion request

We aim to delete or anonymize account-related Mappy cloud data after a valid deletion request, except where limited information must be retained for reasons such as:

- legal or regulatory obligations;
- accounting or transaction records;
- fraud prevention;
- security and abuse prevention;
- resolving disputes or enforcing legal rights.

Third-party service providers may also retain certain records independently under their own policies and legal obligations.
