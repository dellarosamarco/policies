# Loved — Child Safety Standards

**Last updated: November 9, 2026**

Loved is a dating app for adults only. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and for any content or behaviour that sexualises, grooms or endangers minors.

## Age requirement

- Loved may be used only by people aged **18 or older**.
- Every user must enter a date of birth at sign-up; accounts under 18 cannot be created.
- Users must confirm they are adults and accept the [Terms of Service](https://github.com/dellarosamarco/policies/blob/main/loved-terms-of-service.md) before using the app.
- If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we close it.

## Prohibited content and behaviour

It is strictly forbidden to use Loved to:

- share, request or promote child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any sexual content involving minors;
- groom, solicit or attempt to contact minors;
- sexualise minors in profiles, photos or messages;
- facilitate trafficking or exploitation of any person.

## How we prevent and detect abuse

- **In-app reporting:** any user can report a profile or a conversation from the "..." button on the profile card or in the chat. "Minor" is a dedicated report reason.
- **Immediate action on minor reports:** a profile reported as belonging to a minor is flagged for review immediately, without waiting for multiple reports.
- **Blocking:** users can block anyone; blocked users can no longer contact them or see their profile.
- **Automatic photo moderation:** every uploaded photo is checked automatically; explicit or violent images are removed.
- **Evidence preservation:** reports filed from a chat preserve the latest messages so moderators can review them.
- **Human moderation:** moderators review flagged profiles and suspend or permanently ban accounts that violate these standards.
- **Restricted communication:** users can message each other only after a mutual match.

## Reporting to authorities

When we become aware of CSAM or of a child at risk, we remove the content, ban the account, preserve relevant information as permitted by law, and report it to the competent authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and the Italian Postal and Communications Police (Polizia Postale), in accordance with applicable laws.

## Compliance

Loved complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations, including Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.
