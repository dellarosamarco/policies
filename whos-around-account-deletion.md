# Who's Around — Account and Data Deletion

**Last updated: October 16, 2026**

This page explains how to delete your Who's Around account and the data associated with it.

## Delete from the app

1. Open **Who's Around** and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile** → **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete account** at the bottom of the page.
4. Confirm, entering your password again if asked.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. It removes:

- your account (Firebase Authentication user);
- your profile and profile photo;
- the events you created, with their chats and join requests;
- your participations and join requests in other people's events;
- your private chats; in group chats your messages are shown as deleted and your photos removed;
- your saved events, notifications, blocks and device registrations;
- the ratings you gave or received and the notifications that mention you;
- premium entitlement data stored by Who's Around.

## Subscriptions and purchases

Deleting your account does **not automatically cancel a Google Play or Apple App Store subscription**. Manage or cancel it from your store account. Google and Apple may keep purchase records under their own policies.

## If you cannot access the app

Request deletion by e-mail at **hoenngames@gmail.com** from the address you use to sign in, or include enough information to identify your account. Do **not** send your password. We may need to verify that you own the account before deleting it.

## What may be retained

Limited information may be retained where required by law, to resolve disputes, or to prevent fraud and abuse (for example, the reports you filed and those other users filed about the account, which moderators need to review). Data held independently by Google, Apple or other providers is governed by their own policies.
