# Who's Around — Community Guidelines

Who's Around works when everyone feels safe. These rules apply to profiles, events, messages and meetups.

## Be real

- Use your name and photos that represent you.
- Describe the event as it is: where, when, what you'll do.
- No fake profiles, spam or advertising.

## Be respectful

- No harassment, insults, threats or discrimination.
- No means no: if someone doesn't accept your request or doesn't reply, let it go.
- No sexually explicit content or insistent requests.

## Stay safe

- Meet people in public places, at least the first times.
- Tell someone you trust where you're going.
- Don't share sensitive data (home address, documents, bank details).
- Never send money to people you met in the App.
- If you are in danger, call the emergency services.

## Not allowed

- Illegal or dangerous activities, drugs, weapons.
- Paid meetups or sexual services.
- Commercial events disguised as meetups between people.
- Minors: the App is for adults only.

## Report and block

If something is wrong use **Report** or **Block** from the profile, the event or the chat. People you block can no longer send you requests or messages. Accounts reported by several people are suspended and reviewed by our team.
