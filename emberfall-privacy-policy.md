# Privacy Policy – Emberfall Online

**Last updated:** November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Emberfall Online** ("the Game", "we", "us"), developed by MaarcoDev, collects, uses and protects information when you play the Game on Android.

Emberfall Online is an online multiplayer game: to play, your device connects to our game servers, where you meet, chat and play with other players in real time.

## 1. Who we are

Developer: MaarcoDev
Contact: **hoenngames@gmail.com**

## 2. Information we collect

We do **not** ask for your real name, phone number, location, contacts or photos. When you start the Game, an **anonymous account** is created automatically. You can optionally link it to your Google account to recover your character on another device.

| Data | Purpose | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous user ID, Firebase installation ID and (optional) Google account ID/email used only for sign-in | Account, login, cloud save | Google Firebase (Authentication, Installations) |
| Character name you choose, character progress (level, class, skills, items, gold, quests, friends, guild, settings) | Cloud save, multiplayer features | Google Cloud Firestore and our game server |
| In-game chat messages (public, party, guild and private) | Delivering messages to other players, moderation, handling reports | Our game server |
| IP address, connection times, gameplay events (position, combat, trades) | Running the real-time multiplayer game, preventing cheating, fraud and abuse | Our game server |
| App interactions (screens visited, features used) | Improving balance, content and performance | Google Analytics for Firebase |
| Crash logs and diagnostics (device model, OS version, stack traces) | Fixing bugs and improving stability | Firebase Crashlytics |
| Advertising ID and ad interaction data (only if you choose to watch optional rewarded ads) | Showing rewarded ads, measuring ad performance | Google AdMob |
| Purchase confirmations (product ID, purchase token) | Delivering in-app purchases | Google Play Billing |

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details.

## 3. How we use information

- To create your account and let you log in and recover your character
- To run the shared online world: showing your character, name and actions to other players
- To deliver chat messages and to review reported messages and players
- To detect and prevent cheating, exploits, fraud and abusive behavior
- To deliver items you purchase
- To understand how the Game is played and improve it
- To detect and fix crashes and errors

We do **not** sell your personal data.

## 4. Information visible to other players

Your **character name**, appearance, level, guild, online status and the messages you send in public, party or guild chat are visible to other players. Private messages are visible to the recipient. Do not share personal information (such as your real name, address or phone number) in chat.

## 5. Advertising

The Game may offer **optional rewarded ads** (for example, to receive a bonus after watching an ad). Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings. Where required (for example in the EU/EEA and UK), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.

## 6. Data sharing

Data is processed only by us and by the service providers listed above, which act on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

- Google (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play): https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Our game server is hosted by a cloud hosting provider located in the European Union, which stores data on our behalf. We do not share your data with any other third party, except where required by law or to protect the safety of players.

## 7. Data retention and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS). Character data is kept while your account is active. Chat messages and server logs (IP address, gameplay events) are kept for up to **90 days** for moderation and anti-cheat purposes, unless they are needed longer to handle a report or a legal obligation. Analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase default retention periods.

## 8. Your rights and data deletion

You can request access to, or deletion of, your data at any time from the in-game Settings or by following the instructions on the [Emberfall Online Account and Data Deletion page](emberfall-account-deletion.md), or by writing to **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 9. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

## 11. Contact
