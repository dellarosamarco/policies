# Privacy Policy - Calisthenics Ranking: Level Up

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Calisthenics Ranking: Level Up mobile app ("the App"), published on Google Play by Workout Master, collects, uses and protects your information.

## 1. Information we collect

**Data stored on your device.** Your profile (nickname, optional sex, body weight, training days), max-rep test results, workouts, XP, quests, achievements and settings are stored locally on your device. The App works offline.

**Account data (Firebase Authentication).** The App creates an anonymous account to sync your progress. If you choose to link Google or Apple Sign-In, we receive your account identifier and email address from that provider.

**Cloud backup and leaderboards (Cloud Firestore).** If online features are available, your nickname, avatar, rank, level, XP and benchmark results are stored in Google Firebase to provide backup, restore and the global and weekly leaderboards. Your nickname, avatar, rank and XP are visible to other users on leaderboards.

**Analytics and crash reports.** We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to fix crashes. These services may collect device information, app interactions, crash logs and an app-instance identifier. They do not collect your name or contact details.

**Purchases.** Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details; we only receive the purchase status needed to unlock Premium.

**Notifications.** Workout and streak reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can disable them at any time.

The App does not show advertising and does not sell your personal data.

## 2. How we use information

- To provide the App's features (ranking, training, quests, progression)
- - To back up and restore your progress across devices
  - - To display leaderboards
    - - To unlock and verify Premium purchases
      - - To improve stability and performance
