# Privacy Policy - Calisthenics Ranking: Level Up

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Calisthenics Ranking: Level Up mobile app ("the App"), published on Google Play by Workout Master, collects, uses and protects your information.

## 1. Information we collect

**Data stored on your device.** Your profile (nickname, optional sex, body weight, training days), max-rep test results, workouts, XP, quests, achievements and settings are stored locally on your device. The App works offline.

**Account data (Firebase Authentication).** The App creates an anonymous account to sync your progress. If you choose to link Google or Apple Sign-In, we receive your account identifier and email address from that provider.

**Cloud backup and leaderboards (Cloud Firestore).** If online features are available, your nickname, avatar, rank, level, XP and benchmark results are stored in Google Firebase to provide backup, restore and the global and weekly leaderboards. Your nickname, avatar, rank and XP are visible to other users on leaderboards.

**Analytics and crash reports.** We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to fix crashes. These services may collect device information, app interactions, crash logs and an app-instance identifier. They do not collect your name or contact details.

**Usage analytics (PostHog).** We also use PostHog, a product analytics service, to understand how the App is used. When your device is online, the App sends PostHog:

- a random identifier created by the App on installation (it is not your advertising ID and is not linked to your name, e-mail or account);
- usage events: app installed, updated, opened and closed, screens viewed, onboarding steps, workouts started, completed or abandoned (workout type, number of sets, duration, XP earned and how the workout felt), max tests and rank-ups, achievements, features used (such as programs, custom workouts, quests, skills, leaderboard and data export), daily quest bonuses, reminders opened, Premium screens viewed, purchases and restores (product, price and currency), shares, rating prompts and account linking (only the provider, e.g. Google or Apple);
- a few progress properties: rank, level, Premium status and total number of workouts;
- device and app information (device manufacturer and model, operating system and version, app version, language, screen size, time zone and network type);
- your IP address, which PostHog uses to derive an approximate location (country, region, city).

PostHog does not record your screen (no session replay) and does not receive your nickname, e-mail, workout titles or advertising ID. Events are linked only to the random identifier above; no personal profile is created in PostHog.

**Purchases.** Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details; we only receive the purchase status needed to unlock Premium.

**Notifications.** Workout and streak reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can disable them at any time.

The App does not show advertising and does not sell your personal data.

## 2. How we use information

- To provide the App's features (ranking, training, quests, progression)
- To back up and restore your progress across devices
- To display leaderboards
- To unlock and verify Premium purchases
- To improve stability and performance
- To understand how the App is used and improve it: measuring retention, finding where users get stuck or drop off, and fixing problems (usage analytics). Analytics data is not used for advertising and is never sold.

**Legal basis for analytics.** Where the GDPR applies, we process usage analytics and crash reports (Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and PostHog) on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding how the App is used, keeping it stable and improving it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You have the right to object to this processing at any time (see section 4).

## 3. Sharing

We share data only with service providers that operate the App:

- Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics) and Google Play Billing. Their use of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- PostHog, Inc. (USA) — product analytics — https://posthog.com/privacy

**International transfers.** PostHog, Inc. is based in the USA and analytics data is stored in PostHog's US cloud. These transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (included in PostHog's Data Processing Agreement) and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. You can export your data or permanently delete your account and cloud data from Settings > Account inside the App. You can also request deletion by email (see section 7); we will delete your cloud data within 30 days.

PostHog analytics events are kept for up to 12 months and are then deleted automatically or anonymised/aggregated.

**Stopping analytics.** The App currently has no in-app setting to turn off usage analytics. Analytics stop when you uninstall the App, and clearing the App's data or reinstalling it resets the random analytics identifier. To object to analytics or to request deletion of analytics data, write to hoenngames@gmail.com.

## 5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by Firebase security rules.

## 6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Analytics data is pseudonymous and is not used for advertising or profiling.
