# Privacy Policy – LifeRoll: Life Simulator

**Last updated:** November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **LifeRoll: Life Simulator** ("the Game", "we", "us"), developed by MaarcoDev (Google Play developer account "Workout Master"), handles information when you play the Game.

## 1. Who we are

Developer: MaarcoDev
Contact: **hoenngames@gmail.com**

## 2. Information we collect

The Game can be played **offline** and does **not** require an account. We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising ID. The Game **contains no advertising** and no advertising or tracking SDKs. When your device is online, it sends **pseudonymous usage analytics** to PostHog (see below). Any name you type for your fictional character is stored only on your device and is never sent.

| Data | Purpose | Where it is stored / processed |
|---|---|---|
| Game progress (your simulated lives and save slots, family tree, achievements and ribbons, challenges, currency, settings, language). Characters are fictional and randomly generated; no real personal data is required | Saving your progress | Only locally on your device |
| Purchase confirmations (product ID, purchase token, order status) | Delivering and restoring in-app purchases | Google Play Billing |
| Usage analytics: a random device identifier, gameplay and usage events, device and app information, IP address (used for approximate location) | Understanding how the Game is played and improving it | PostHog (USA) |

**Usage analytics (PostHog).** The Game uses PostHog, a product analytics service. It sends:

- a random identifier created on installation (it is not your advertising ID and is not linked to your name or email);
- usage events: game installed, updated, opened and closed; screens viewed; tutorial steps; lives started, aged up and ended (life stage, game mode, age reached, generation, cause of death, play time); scenarios, daily and weekly challenges and achievements completed; daily rewards; features used (such as tabs, minigames and their score, revives and gem uses, settings changes); shop views and purchases started, completed or failed (product, price and currency); notification permission and notifications opened; rating prompts; and error events (where the error happened and a short error message);
- a few stable properties: premium status, number of lives played, highest generation, number of achievements and best streak;
- device and app information (device manufacturer and model, operating system and version, app version, language, screen size, time zone and network type);
- your IP address, which PostHog uses to derive an approximate location (country, region, city).

There is **no session replay or screen recording**, and no names, email or advertising ID are sent. Events are linked only to the random identifier above; no personal profile is created in PostHog.

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details.

## 3. How we use information

- To save your game progress on your device
- To deliver items you purchase and restore purchases
- To understand how the Game is played and improve it: measuring retention, finding where players get stuck or drop off, and fixing problems (usage analytics)

We do **not** sell your personal data, we share it only with the service providers listed in section 6, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

**Legal basis.** If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, we process usage analytics on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding how the Game is played and improving it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You have the right to object at any time (see section 7).

## 4. Notifications

The Game may show optional **local notifications** (for example when your daily reward or daily challenge is ready). They are generated on your device, are only shown if you allow them, and can be disabled at any time in the Game settings or in your device settings.

## 5. In-app review

The Game may ask you to rate it using the Google Play In-App Review API. The review is handled entirely by Google Play; we do not receive your identity.

## 6. Third-party services

- Google Play / Google Play Billing / In-App Review: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- PostHog, Inc. (USA) — product analytics — https://posthog.com/privacy

PostHog, Inc. is based in the USA and analytics data is stored in PostHog's US cloud. These transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (included in PostHog's Data Processing Agreement) and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

## 7. Data retention, security and deletion

Game data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear the app data. The Game has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. For any request, write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

PostHog usage analytics events are kept for up to 12 months and are then deleted automatically or anonymised/aggregated.

**Stopping analytics.** The Game currently has no in-game setting to turn off usage analytics. Analytics stop when you uninstall the Game, and clearing the app data or reinstalling resets the random analytics identifier. To object to usage analytics or to request deletion of analytics data, write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are a California resident, you have the rights granted by the CCPA/CPRA; we do not sell or share personal information.

## 8. Children

The Game contains mature themes (such as fictional crime, relationships and simulated gambling without real money) and is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Usage analytics data is pseudonymous and is not used for advertising or profiling. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
