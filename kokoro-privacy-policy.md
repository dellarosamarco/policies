# Kokoro – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 2, 2026* · [Italiano](kokoro-privacy-policy-it.md)

This policy explains what data the Kokoro app ("Kokoro", "we") processes, why, and your rights. The data controller is **Marco Della Rosa**, contact: [hoenngames@gmail.com](mailto:hoenngames@gmail.com).

## 1. Data we process

- **Account identifier**: created by Firebase Authentication when you start. If you play as a guest, the account is anonymous and we do not know your name, e-mail or phone number.
- **Google account data** (only if you sign in with Google): your e-mail address, name and profile picture, received from Google to identify your account and restore your progress on other devices.
- **Chat content**: the messages you send to the characters and their AI-generated replies, with timestamps, mood and bond level.
- **Nickname** (optional): stored on your device and sent with your messages so characters can call you by name.
- **Wallet data**: coin balance and transaction history (welcome bonus, daily rewards, messages, refunds, purchases).
- **Purchase data**: Google Play order ID, product and purchase token, used only to verify and credit purchases. We never receive your payment card details.
- **Reports**: when you report an AI message, we store the message text and the reason you selected.
- **Technical logs**: error logs from our servers, kept for a limited time to keep the service running.

## 2. Why we process it (legal bases)

- To provide the service you request: chat replies, wallet, purchases (performance of a contract).
- To keep the service safe, prevent abuse and review reported content (legitimate interest; legal obligations).
- To comply with tax and accounting rules for purchases (legal obligation).

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising and we do not use your conversations to train AI models.

## 3. Service providers

- **Google Firebase** (Google LLC / Google Ireland): authentication, database, cloud functions.
- **Google Cloud Vertex AI** (Gemini models, via Firebase): generates the characters' replies. Your recent messages with a character, the character's persona and your nickname are sent to Gemini to generate each reply. Under Google Cloud's terms, this data is not used to train Google's models.
- **Google Play**: payment processing for coin packs.

Some providers process data outside the EU/EEA (e.g. in the United States) under appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## 4. Retention

We keep your data until you delete it. You can delete everything at any time in the app (*Me → Delete my data*) or by following the instructions in [Kokoro – Account deletion](kokoro-account-deletion.md). Purchase records may be retained as long as required by law. Reports are kept for up to 12 months.

## 5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. under the GDPR) you have the right to access, rectify, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data and to data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy: the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). To exercise your rights write to [hoenngames@gmail.com](mailto:hoenngames@gmail.com), including the wallet address shown in the app.

## 6. Age requirement

Kokoro is intended only for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly process data of minors. If you believe a minor is using the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

## 7. AI-generated content

All characters are fictional and their replies are generated by artificial intelligence. Replies may be inaccurate and do not constitute professional advice. Do not share sensitive personal information in chats.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app.
