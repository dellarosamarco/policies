# Privacy Policy – Tile Odyssey: Triple Match

**Last updated:** November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Tile Odyssey: Triple Match** ("the Game", "we", "us"), developed by MaarcoDev (Google Play developer account "Workout Master"), handles information when you play the Game.

## 1. Who we are

Developer: MaarcoDev
Contact: **hoenngames@gmail.com**

## 2. Information we collect

The Game is designed to work **offline** and does **not** require an account. We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising ID, and the Game **contains no advertising**. When your device is online, the Game sends pseudonymous **usage analytics** to PostHog (see below).

| Data | Purpose | Where it is stored / processed |
|---|---|---|
| Game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, collections, settings, language) | Saving your progress | Only locally on your device |
| Purchase confirmations (product ID, purchase token, order status) | Delivering and restoring in-app purchases | Google Play Billing |
| Crash and diagnostic data (only if enabled in a future version: device model, OS version, stack traces) | Fixing bugs and improving stability | Google Firebase Crashlytics |
| Usage analytics: random device identifier, usage events, device and app information, IP address (used for approximate location) | Understanding how the Game is used and improving it | PostHog (PostHog, Inc., USA), stored in PostHog's US cloud |

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details.

**Usage analytics (PostHog).** The Game uses **PostHog** (PostHog, Inc., USA) for product analytics. PostHog processes:

- a **random device identifier** created by the PostHog software when the Game is first started; it is not the advertising ID and is not linked to your name or email;
- **usage events**: the Game being installed, updated, opened and sent to the background; screens opened; levels started, completed or failed (level number or game mode, level type, duration, score, stars, matches, boosters used, continues, reason for failing); tutorial levels completed; features used (for example boosters, continues, lives refills, star chests, missions, event rewards, tile skins, settings, language, restoring purchases, rating the Game); achievements and player levels reached; daily rewards; running out of lives; shop views and purchases (product ID, price and currency); rating prompts; notifications opened; errors (where they happened and a short technical message);
- a few **game properties** used to group players: highest level reached, player level and whether you own the Premium Pass;
- **device and app information** added automatically: device manufacturer and model, operating system and version, app version and build, language, screen size, time zone and network type;
- your **IP address**, which PostHog uses to derive an **approximate location** (country, region, city).

PostHog does **not** record your screen (no session replay) and receives no names, email addresses or advertising ID. Events are linked only to the random device identifier; no PostHog user profile is created. Analytics data is not used for advertising and is never sold.

**Legal basis and transfers (EU/EEA and UK).** Usage analytics are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding how the Game is used and improving it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you have the right to object at any time. PostHog, Inc. is based in the United States, and analytics data is stored in PostHog's US cloud. These transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (included in PostHog's Data Processing Agreement) and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

**How to stop analytics.** The Game currently has **no in-app setting** to turn off analytics. Uninstalling the Game stops all collection, and clearing the app data or reinstalling resets the random identifier (clearing data also deletes your local progress). To object to analytics or request deletion of analytics data, write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

## 3. How we use information

- To save your game progress on your device
- To deliver items you purchase and restore purchases
- To detect and fix crashes and errors (if diagnostics are enabled)
- To understand how the Game is used and improve it: measure how many players come back, find levels where players get stuck or stop playing, and fix problems (usage analytics)

We do **not** sell your personal data, we share it only with the service providers listed in section 6, and we do not use it (including analytics data) for advertising.

## 4. Notifications

The Game may show optional **local notifications** (for example when your lives are full or a new daily challenge is available). They are generated on your device and can be disabled at any time in the Game settings or in your device settings.

## 5. In-app review

The Game may ask you to rate it using the Google Play In-App Review API. The review is handled entirely by Google Play; we do not receive your identity.

## 6. Third-party services

- Google Play / Google Play Billing: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase (only if diagnostics are enabled): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- PostHog, Inc. (USA) — product analytics: https://posthog.com/privacy

## 7. Data retention, security and deletion

Game data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear the app data. The Game has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Usage analytics events are stored by PostHog, linked only to a random device identifier, and are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted automatically (or anonymised and aggregated). For any request, write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 8. Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Analytics data is pseudonymous and is not used for advertising or profiling. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
