# Privacy Policy – Tile Odyssey: Triple Match

**Last updated:** November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Tile Odyssey: Triple Match** ("the Game", "we", "us"), developed by MaarcoDev (Google Play developer account "Workout Master"), handles information when you play the Game.

## 1. Who we are

Developer: MaarcoDev
Contact: **hoenngames@gmail.com**

## 2. Information we collect

The Game works offline and does **not** require an account. We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or advertising ID, and the Game **contains no advertising**.

| Data | Purpose | Where it is stored / processed |
|---|---|---|
| Game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, collections, settings, language) | Saving your progress | Only locally on your device |
| Anonymous app activity (screens viewed, levels started/completed/failed, features used, purchase events without payment details) and an anonymous random device identifier generated by the analytics SDK | Understanding how the Game is played, improving balance, content and stability | PostHog Inc. (USA), analytics processor |
| Purchase confirmations (product ID, purchase token, order status) | Delivering and restoring in-app purchases | Google Play Billing |

Analytics data is pseudonymous: it is not linked to your name, email or Google account and we never send personal data or free text to PostHog. Analytics is sent only when the device is online.

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details.

## 3. How we use information

- To save your game progress on your device
- To deliver items you purchase and restore purchases
- To measure aggregate usage (retention, level difficulty, where players get stuck) and improve the Game

We do **not** sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Notifications

The Game may show optional **local notifications** (for example when your lives are full or a new daily challenge is available). They are generated on your device and can be disabled at any time in the Game settings or in your device settings.

## 5. In-app review

The Game may ask you to rate it using the Google Play In-App Review API. The review is handled entirely by Google Play; we do not receive your identity.

## 6. Third-party services

- Google Play / Google Play Billing: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- PostHog (analytics): https://posthog.com/privacy

These providers act as processors on our behalf. We do not share your data with any other third party, except where required by law.

## 7. Data retention, security and deletion

Game data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear the app data. Analytics data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and retained by PostHog for a limited period according to our project settings. Because analytics data is not linked to an account, you can stop all future collection by uninstalling the Game; to request deletion of analytics data associated with your device, write to **hoenngames@gmail.com**.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 8. Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

## 10. Contact

For any question about this Privacy Policy: **hoenngames@gmail.com**
